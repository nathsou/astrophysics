Put two structures side by side and list sentences: each structure makes every one of them true or false (its theory is complete). A single sentence on which they differ shows that they are not elementarily equivalent; agreeing on a list shows nothing about all sentences. The second structure starts as “ℕ cut off at 4”; switch to “M with its elements renamed” to see an isomorphic copy.

<Added label="Added for this edition">

$\mathfrak N$ has two pleasant features: its domain is $\mathbb N$, and its operations are computable (and $<$ decidable). A structure for $\mathcal L_A$ is **computable** if its domain is $\mathbb N$ and $'$, $+$, $\times$ are computable functions and $<$ a decidable relation. Can a non-standard model be computable?

</Added>

## For Q: yes

The model $\mathbf K$ of $\mathbf Q$ has domain $\mathbb N \cup \{a\}$, which is countable. Relabel: let the number $0$ play the role of $a$ and $n + 1$ the role of $n$. The book spells out the resulting structure $\mathbf K'$ with domain $\mathbb N$ (<Ref k="mod:mar:cmp:ex:comp-model-q" />): $0^{\mathbf K'} = 1$; ${}'^{\mathbf K'}$ sends $0$ to $0$ and $n > 0$ to $n + 1$; $+^{\mathbf K'}(x, y) = x + y - 1$ for $x, y > 0$ (and $0$ otherwise); and so on. All of these are computable. So $\mathbf Q$ has a computable non-standard model; the relabeling is an isomorphism from $\mathbf K'$ to $\mathbf K$, so $\mathbf K'$ satisfies $\mathbf Q$ as well.

## For PA: no

**Tennenbaum’s theorem**: $\mathfrak N$ is the only computable model of $\mathbf{PA}$. A countable non-standard model of $\mathbf{PA}$ can be given the domain $\mathbb N$, just as $\mathbf K$ was — but then its interpretations of $+$ and $\times$ cannot be computable functions (together with a decidable $<$ and computable $'$). Non-standard models of $\mathbf{PA}$ exist, but no algorithm can calculate in one.

The book states the theorem without proof. The standard proof (not in this book) uses a pair of computably inseparable sets: in a non-standard model of $\mathbf{PA}$ a single non-standard element codes a set of numbers separating them, and if $+$ and $\times$ were computable, that set could be computed.

## Why this matters

This is why the non-standard models of <Ref k="mod:mar:nst:sec" /> and <Ref k="mod:mar:mpa:sec" /> are described rather than computed with, and why the picture in <Ref k="mod:mar:mpa:sec" /> shows $\prec$ but not $\oplus$ and $\otimes$: the order of a countable non-standard model of $\mathbf{PA}$ can be given by a decidable relation on $\mathbb N$, but its addition and multiplication cannot both be computable.

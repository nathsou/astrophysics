The formulas for “$s(X)$ is infinite”, “$s(X)$ is countable” and “$s(X)$ has size $\aleph_0$ or $\aleph_1$”, on a small domain. Choose $X$ in the assignment: on a finite domain Inf(X) is false and Count(X) true for every non-empty $X$, and so Aleph₀(X) and Aleph₁(X) are false for every $X$.

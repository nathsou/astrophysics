So far, your sentences have mostly stood alone. Real conversation links them: I'm not coming *because* I'm ill; it's expensive, *but* it's worth it. This lesson teaches the linking words, through a situation where you'll want them most: feeling unwell, far from home.

## The body, and what hurts

```words
身体 | body; health
头 | head
眼睛 | eye(s)
手 | hand
疼 | to hurt, sore
舒服 | comfortable; well
药 | medicine
药店 | pharmacy
```

"My head hurts" is 我头疼, literally "I head hurts": the person, then the body part, then 疼. 不舒服 (not comfortable) is the all-purpose "I don't feel well".

```dialogue
title: At the pharmacy
店员: 你好，你哪儿不舒服？ | Hello, what's wrong? (where are you uncomfortable?)
马克: 我头疼，眼睛也有点儿疼。 | I have a headache, and my eyes hurt a bit too.
店员: 你发烧吗？ | Do you have a fever?
马克: 没有，可能是因为我太累了。 | No, maybe it's because I'm too tired.
店员: 这个药很好。一天三次，一次一片。 | This medicine is good. Three times a day, one tablet each time.
马克: 好，谢谢。 | OK, thanks.
店员: 不客气。你要好好休息！ | You're welcome. Get plenty of rest!
```

:::culture
Chinese pharmacies sell both Western medicine and traditional Chinese medicine (中药), often side by side, and pharmacists will happily recommend something for minor ailments. For anything more serious, people go to a hospital outpatient department rather than a family doctor: GP surgeries, as Britain knows them, are rare.
:::

## Because…, so…: 因为…所以…

Chinese happily uses **both** halves together, where English would pick one:

> **因为**今天下雨，**所以**我不去了。 Because it's raining today, (so) I'm not going.

Either half can also stand alone: 我不去了，因为下雨 / 下雨了，所以我不去. The cause usually comes first, which feels natural once you're used to it: reason, then result.

```words
因为 | because
所以 | so, therefore
```

## Although…, but…: 虽然…但是…

Same idea, same doubling: 虽然 (although) pairs with 但是 or 可是 (but).

> **虽然**汉语很难，**但是**很有意思。 Although Chinese is hard, it's very interesting.

但 is a shorter, slightly more formal 但是.

```words
虽然 | although
但是 | but
但 | but
就 | then; right away; just
```

## Maybe, and hopefully

```words
可能 | maybe, possibly
希望 | to hope
告诉 | to tell
意思 | meaning
懂 | to understand
让 | to let; to make (someone do)
不好意思 | sorry; excuse me (embarrassed)
坏 | bad; broken
```

- 可能 before the verb is "maybe": 他可能不来了 (he may not come).
- 希望 is "hope": 我希望你身体好 (I hope you're well).
- 让 is "let, make (someone do)": 医生让我多休息 (the doctor told me to rest more), 让我看看 (let me have a look).
- 不好意思 is the "sorry" of small embarrassments: bumping into someone, asking a favour, being late. 对不起 is for real apologies.

```choose
title: What do you say?
items:
  - prompt: You're five minutes late for coffee with a friend.
    options: [不好意思，我来晚了。, 没关系，我来晚了。, 不客气，我来晚了。]
    answer: 0
  - prompt: You didn't understand what someone said.
    options: [对不起，我不懂。, 我不认识。, 我不知道你。]
    answer: 0
  - prompt: "\"The doctor told me to drink more water\""
    options: [医生让我多喝水。, 医生告诉我多喝水。, Both are fine.]
    answer: 2
    explain: 让 (made me) and 告诉 (told me) both work here.
  - prompt: "\"What does this word mean?\""
    options: [这个词是什么意思？, 这个词懂什么？, 这个词意思吗？]
    answer: 0
```

```scene
title: The doctor's
setting: A clinic in Shanghai. You've had a bad cold for three days.
partner: 医生
turns:
  - they: 你好，请坐。你哪儿不舒服？
    theyEn: Hello, take a seat. What's the trouble?
    options:
      - zh: 我头疼，身体也很不舒服。
        en: I have a headache, and I feel unwell all over.
        ok: true
      - zh: 我很舒服，谢谢。
        en: I'm very comfortable, thanks.
        reply: 那……你为什么来医院？
        replyEn: Then… why have you come to the hospital?
  - they: 疼了多长时间了？
    theyEn: How long has it hurt?
    options:
      - zh: 三天了。
        en: Three days.
        ok: true
      - zh: 三点了。
        en: It's three o'clock.
        reply: 我知道现在几点……我问你疼了几天？
        replyEn: I know what time it is… I'm asking how many days it's hurt.
  - they: 你可能是因为太累了，所以感冒了。我给你开一点儿药。
    theyEn: You've probably caught a cold because you're overtired. I'll prescribe some medicine.
    options:
      - zh: 好的。我要吃几次？
        en: OK. How many times do I take it?
        ok: true
      - zh: 虽然我累。
        en: Although I'm tired.
        reply: 虽然……但是？你想说什么？
        replyEn: Although… but? What did you want to say?
  - they: 一天两次。多喝水，多休息。希望你快点儿好！
    theyEn: Twice a day. Drink lots of water and rest. Hope you're better soon!
    options:
      - zh: 谢谢医生！
        en: Thank you, doctor!
        ok: true
end: Prescription in hand, you head to the pharmacy downstairs.
```

```compose
task: Explain why you couldn't do something yesterday, with a reason.
target: 因为…所以…, or 虽然…但是…
examples:
  - 因为我生病了，所以昨天没去上课。
  - 虽然我很累，但是我还是去上班了。
```

```roleplay
title: Feeling unwell
setting: You wake up with a sore throat and headache in your hotel in Beijing, and go down to the pharmacy next door.
partner: 药店店员, a pharmacist who asks what's wrong, for how long, and recommends medicine with instructions
goal: Explain what hurts and for how long, give a possible reason, and understand how to take the medicine.
opener: 你好！你哪儿不舒服？
openerEn: Hello! What's the trouble?
words: [疼, 不舒服, 因为, 所以, 可能, 药, 次]
checks: [said what hurts, said how long, gave a reason with 因为 or 可能, asked or understood how to take the medicine]
```

:::key
- Person + body part + 疼: 我头疼. 不舒服 = unwell.
- 因为…所以… (reason → result) and 虽然…但是… (although → but): both halves are normal together.
- 可能 = maybe; 希望 = hope; 让 = let / make someone do.
- 不好意思 for small embarrassments, 对不起 for real apologies.
:::

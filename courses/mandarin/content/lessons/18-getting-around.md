China's cities are built for getting around: metros that put London's to shame, high-speed trains at 350 km/h, shared bikes on every corner, and taxis summoned by app. This lesson gets you from A to B, and lets you complain about how long it took.

## From A to B: 从…到…

从 (cóng, from) and 到 (dào, to) frame a journey, and as always in Chinese, the route comes before the verb:

- 我**从**家**到**学校走路去。 I walk from home to school.
- **从**北京**到**上海坐火车要五个小时。 From Beijing to Shanghai by train takes five hours.

They work for time too: 从九点到五点 (from nine to five).

```words
从 | from
到 | to; to arrive
路 | road; route
路上 | on the way
走路 | to walk
跑 | to run
飞 | to fly
往 | towards
```

## How far? 离

离 (lí) is "away from", for distances. Note the word order: the place you measure from comes after 离.

- 我家**离**学校很近。 My home is close to school. (home away-from school very near)
- 机场**离**这儿远吗？ Is the airport far from here?
- 我家离公司有十公里。 My home is ten kilometres from work.

```words
离 | away from (distance)
远 | far
近 | near
多远 | how far
```

:::mistake
English says "far **from** here", so learners reach for 从: 机场从这儿很远 ✗. For distance it's always 离: 机场离这儿很远 ✓. 从 is for journeys and starting points, 离 for how far apart.
:::

## How long? Duration after the verb

To say how long something takes or lasts, the duration goes **after** the verb, the opposite of a point in time:

- 我**每天八点**起床。 (point in time: before the verb)
- 我**睡了八个小时**。 I slept for eight hours. (duration: after the verb)
- 坐地铁**要**二十分钟。 It takes twenty minutes by metro. (要 = it takes)

The question is 多长时间 (how long), also after the verb or with 要: 你学了多长时间汉语？ How long have you studied Chinese?

```words
多长时间 | how long (time)
地铁 | metro, subway
公交车 | bus
打车 | to take a taxi
机场 | airport
车站 | station, stop
站 | station; to stand
票 | ticket
机票 | plane ticket
门票 | entrance ticket
```

```order

```

## Directions

```words
左 | left
右 | right
左边 | left side
右边 | right side
前面 | in front, ahead
后面 | behind
旁边 | next to
上面 | on top, above
下面 | below, under
里面 | inside
外面 | outside
楼 | building; floor
门口 | entrance, doorway
洗手间 | toilet, washroom
```

Directions are verb phrases with 往 (towards): 往左走 (go left), 往前走 (go straight ahead), 往右拐 (turn right). Floors are counted with 楼: 三楼 is the third floor (in China, the ground floor is 一楼).

```dialogue
title: Where's the toilet?
马克: 请问，洗手间在哪儿？ | Excuse me, where's the toilet?
服务员: 往前走，在电梯旁边。 | Go straight ahead, it's next to the lift.
马克: 在一楼吗？ | Is it on the ground floor?
服务员: 不，在二楼。上楼往右走。 | No, it's on the first floor. Go upstairs and turn right.
马克: 谢谢！ | Thanks!
```

## Travelling

```words
旅游 | to travel (as a tourist)
外国 | foreign country
出国 | to go abroad
酒店 | hotel
```

```story
title: 去西安
zh: 去西安
paragraphs:
  - zh: 上个月，我和朋友去西安旅游了。我们从北京坐高铁去，路上要四个半小时，比坐飞机还方便。
    en: Last month, my friend and I went travelling to Xi'an. We took the high-speed train from Beijing; the journey takes four and a half hours, even more convenient than flying.
  - zh: 我们的酒店离火车站很近，走路只要十分钟。酒店旁边有一个很大的饭店，我们每天晚上都在那儿吃面条儿。
    en: Our hotel was very close to the station, only ten minutes' walk. Next to the hotel there was a big restaurant, and we ate noodles there every evening.
  - zh: 第二天，我们去看兵马俑。兵马俑离西安有点儿远，我们坐了一个小时的公交车。门票有点儿贵，可是非常值得！
    en: On the second day we went to see the Terracotta Army. It's a bit far from Xi'an; we took a one-hour bus ride. The tickets were a bit expensive, but very worth it!
questions:
  - prompt: How did they get to Xi'an?
    options: [By plane, By high-speed train, By bus]
    answer: 1
  - prompt: How far was the hotel from the station?
    options: [Ten minutes' walk, Four and a half hours, An hour by bus]
    answer: 0
  - prompt: What did they think of the Terracotta Army tickets?
    options: [Cheap, Expensive but worth it, Too expensive to buy]
    answer: 1
```

```scene
title: At the ticket office
setting: The ticket office at Beijing South railway station.
partner: 售票员 (ticket seller)
turns:
  - they: 你好，去哪儿？
    theyEn: Hello, where to?
    options:
      - zh: 你好，我要一张去上海的票。
        en: Hello, I'd like a ticket to Shanghai.
        ok: true
      - zh: 我在上海。
        en: I'm in Shanghai.
        reply: 你在上海？这儿是北京啊！
        replyEn: You're in Shanghai? This is Beijing!
  - they: 什么时候的？
    theyEn: For when?
    options:
      - zh: 明天上午十点的。
        en: Tomorrow at 10 am.
        ok: true
      - zh: 要五个小时。
        en: It takes five hours.
        reply: 对，要五个小时。可是你要几点的票？
        replyEn: Yes, it takes five hours. But what time do you want?
  - they: 好。从北京到上海，五百五十三块。
    theyEn: OK. Beijing to Shanghai, 553 kuai.
    options:
      - zh: 好的。请问，从这儿到站台远吗？
        en: OK. Excuse me, is the platform far from here?
        ok: true
      - zh: 太便宜了！
        en: Too cheap!
        reply: 便宜？很多人觉得很贵！
        replyEn: Cheap? Lots of people think it's expensive!
  - they: 不远，往前走，在右边。
    theyEn: Not far, go straight ahead, it's on the right.
    options:
      - zh: 谢谢！
        en: Thanks!
        ok: true
end: Ticket in hand. Tomorrow, Shanghai.
```

```roleplay
setting: You're at your hotel's front desk in Guangzhou and need to get to the airport for a flight this afternoon.

goal: Find out how far the airport is, how long it takes by two different ways, and decide how to go.
opener: 你好！有什么可以帮你？
openerEn: Hello! How can I help?
words: [机场, 离, 远, 多长时间, 地铁, 打车, 要]
checks: [asked how far the airport is, asked how long it takes, chose a way to get there]
```

:::key
- 从 A 到 B for journeys and ranges; the route comes before the verb.
- A 离 B + 远/近 for distance (not 从!).
- Points in time go before the verb; durations after it: 睡了八个小时. "It takes…" is 要.
- Directions: 往 + direction + 走.
:::

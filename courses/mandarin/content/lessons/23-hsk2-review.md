## The last few words

**别** (bié) is "don't", for telling someone not to do something: 别走！ (Don't go!), 别担心 (don't worry). It's a little softer and much more common than 不要 in this sense.

**啊** (a) at the end of a sentence adds warmth or emphasis, a bit like "oh" or "!": 好啊！ (sure!), 你好啊！ (hey there!), 真漂亮啊！ (it's so pretty!). Its sound blends with the syllable before it, so you'll hear *ya*, *wa* or *na*.

**起来** after a verb means "up" (站起来, stand up), and after 看/听, "seems": 这个菜看起来很好吃 (this dish looks delicious).

**时** is a written, shorter form of 的时候 (when): 上课时 (during class).

```words
别 | don’t; other
啊 | (warm, emphatic sentence ending)
起来 | to get up; (after a verb) up; seems
时 | when; time
万 | ten thousand
间 | measure word for rooms
地 | ground, floor; place
面 | side; noodles (wheat)
包 | bag; to wrap
```

## A long story: 一个星期天

This story uses only words from this course. Read it once for the gist, then again, tapping anything you've forgotten.

```story
title: 一个星期天
zh: 一个星期天
paragraphs:
  - zh: 王先生和他的妻子有两个孩子，一个男孩儿，一个女孩儿。男孩儿叫小明，十岁；女孩儿叫小红，六岁。
    en: Mr Wang and his wife have two children, a boy and a girl. The boy is called Xiaoming, he's ten; the girl is called Xiaohong, she's six.
  - zh: 这个星期天，天气非常好，是晴天。早上八点，妈妈说：“大家快起来吧！我们今天去商场买东西。”
    en: This Sunday the weather was lovely, a clear day. At eight in the morning, Mum said, "Everyone get up! We're going to the shopping centre today."
  - zh: 他们坐地铁去商场，路上要半个小时。小明的裤子太小了，妈妈给他买了一条新裤子。小红想要一个书包，可是书包太贵了，三百多块！
    en: They took the metro to the mall; the journey takes half an hour. Xiaoming's trousers were too small, so Mum bought him a new pair. Xiaohong wanted a school bag, but it was too expensive, more than 300 kuai!
  - zh: 爸爸说：“别不高兴。你的生日快要到了，那天爸爸送你一个，好吗？”小红笑了：“好啊！”
    en: "Dad said, \"Don't be sad. Your birthday's coming soon, and on that day I'll give you one, OK?\" Xiaohong smiled: \"OK!\""
  - zh: 中午，他们去了商场旁边的一个小饭馆。爸爸要了一碗牛肉面，妈妈要了米饭和两个菜。孩子们都想喝奶茶，可是妈妈说：“奶茶太甜了，喝绿茶吧。”
    en: At noon they went to a little restaurant next to the mall. Dad ordered a bowl of beef noodles, Mum ordered rice and two dishes. The kids both wanted milk tea, but Mum said, "Milk tea is too sweet; have green tea."
  - zh: 下午，他们去公园玩儿。小明和爸爸踢足球，踢得很高兴。妈妈坐着看书，小红在地上画画。
    en: In the afternoon they went to play in the park. Xiaoming and Dad played football and had a great time. Mum sat reading, and Xiaohong drew pictures on the ground.
  - zh: 晚上回到家，大家都很累，可是也很快乐。小红说：“今天是我最喜欢的星期天！”
    en: Back home in the evening, everyone was tired but happy. Xiaohong said, "Today was my favourite Sunday!"

    answer: 0
  - prompt: What did Dad promise?
    options: [Milk tea, A school bag for her birthday, A trip to the park]
    answer: 1
  - prompt: What did the children drink at lunch?
    options: [Milk tea, Green tea, Coffee]
    answer: 1
```

## Review: Part 4 in one sitting

```choose
title: Grammar check
items:
  - prompt: "\"I've never been to Shanghai\""
    options: [我没去过上海。, 我不去过上海。, 我没去了上海。]
    answer: 0
    explain: 没 + verb + 过 (lesson 16).
  - prompt: "\"He's taller than me\""
    options: [他比我很高。, 他比我高。, 他高比我。]
    answer: 1
    explain: No 很 with 比 (lesson 17).
  - prompt: "\"My home is far from the station\""
    options: [我家从车站很远。, 我家离车站很远。]
    answer: 1
    explain: Distance uses 离 (lesson 18).
  - prompt: "\"I slept for eight hours\""
    options: [我八个小时睡了。, 我睡了八个小时。]
    answer: 1
    explain: Duration goes after the verb (lesson 18).
  - prompt: "\"The door is open\""
    options: [门开着。, 门在开。]
    answer: 0
    explain: 着 for a lasting state (lesson 19).
  - prompt: "\"Although it's expensive, it's good\""
    options: [虽然很贵，但是很好。, 因为很贵，所以很好。]
    answer: 0
    explain: 虽然…但是 (lesson 20).
  - prompt: "\"She sings well\""
    options: [她唱歌得很好。, 她唱歌唱得很好。, 她很好唱歌。]
    answer: 1
    explain: Repeat the verb before 得 when there's an object (lesson 22).
  - prompt: "\"This is a bit expensive\""
    options: [这个有点儿贵。, 这个贵有点儿。, 这个一点儿贵。]
    answer: 0
    explain: 有点儿 before the adjective (lesson 22).
```

## What next?

You've reached the end of the course. Here is how to keep going:

1. **Keep reviewing.** Your deck now holds every HSK 1 and 2 word. A few minutes a day keeps them; skipping a week loses them.
2. **Take the mock exams** in the Mock exam section, and redo the lessons behind any section you miss.
3. **Listen more than you think you need to.** Shadow the dialogues; find a podcast or show for learners and use it every day.
4. **Talk.** A language exchange, a tutor, or the AI conversation partner: every conversation makes the next one easier.

:::key
- 别 = don't; 啊 adds warmth; 起来 = up, or "seems" after 看/听.
- Part 4's patterns: 了 and 过, 比 and 一样, 离 and duration, 在/着/快要…了, 因为/虽然, 吧 and 为什么, 得 and 有点儿/一点儿.
- 加油！ (jiāyóu, "add oil"): keep going!
:::

Chinese time words are numbers in disguise. Monday is "week-one", March is "three-month", and once you can count you can already say most of them. The real lesson here is a principle you will use for the rest of your Chinese life: **big before small, and when before what**.

## What time is it?

```words
现在 | now
点 | o’clock
分 | minute (on the clock)
半 | half
几点 | what time
```

To tell the time, say the hour with 点 (o'clock), then the minutes with 分 (often dropped in speech). Half past is 半, "half".

| Time | Chinese | Literally |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3:00 | 三点 | three o'clock |
| 2:00 | 两点 | two o'clock (两, not 二) |
| 8:30 | 八点半 | eight o'clock half |
| 9:15 | 九点十五（分） | nine o'clock fifteen (minutes) |
| 7:05 | 七点零五（分） | seven o'clock zero five |
| 11:45 | 十一点四十五（分） | eleven o'clock forty-five |

The question is 现在几点？ "Now how-many o'clock?". Note: no 是, no verb at all.

## Parts of the day

Chinese adds the part of the day *before* the time, because it is bigger: 下午三点 is "afternoon three o'clock", 3 pm.

```words
早上 | (early) morning
上午 | morning (until noon)
中午 | noon, midday
下午 | afternoon
晚上 | evening, night
白天 | daytime
```

## Days, weeks, months, years

The days of the week are 星期 (week) plus a number, with Sunday as the exception:

| Monday | Tuesday | Wednesday | Thursday | Friday | Saturday | Sunday |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 星期一 | 星期二 | 星期三 | 星期四 | 星期五 | 星期六 | 星期天 / 星期日 |

Months are a number plus 月: 一月 (January) to 十二月 (December). Dates are a number plus 号 in speech (日 in writing): 十月三号, October the 3rd. Years are read digit by digit plus 年: 二零二六年.

Yesterday, today, tomorrow, and last/this/next year, are all little compounds:

```words
今天 | today
明天 | tomorrow
昨天 | yesterday
天 | day
星期 | week
星期天 | Sunday
星期日 | Sunday (more formal)
月 | month
号 | day of the month (spoken)
日 | day of the month (written)
年 | year
今年 | this year
去年 | last year
明年 | next year
```

:::tip
Notice the pattern: 今 means "this, now", 明 "the next", and 去 (go) "the one that's gone". For weeks and months, Chinese uses up and down instead: 上个星期 is last week (the one above), 下个月 is next month (the one below). Time flows downwards, like a calendar on the wall.
:::

## Big before small

Here is the rule. Whenever Chinese gives a series of time (or place) words, it starts from the biggest unit and zooms in:

> **2026年10月3号星期六上午九点**
> 2026, October, 3rd, Saturday, morning, nine o'clock

English goes back and forth ("9 am on Saturday, 3 October 2026"); Chinese always zooms in. The same goes for addresses (country, city, street, number) and for names (family name first).

```order
title: Zoom in
items:
  - en: 3 pm today
    zh: 今天 下午 三点
  - en: 8:30 on Monday morning
    zh: 星期一 上午 八点半
  - en: October 1st, 2026
    zh: 2026年 10月 1号
  - en: Tomorrow evening at seven
    zh: 明天 晚上 七点
```

## When before what

The second rule: **time words go before the verb**, never at the end. Either before or after the subject:

- 我**明天**去北京。 / **明天**我去北京。 Tomorrow I'm going to Beijing.
- 我们**晚上七点**吃饭。 We eat at 7 in the evening.
- *我去北京明天。* ✗ (sounds as wrong as "I tomorrow go to Beijing" in English, just the other way round)

To ask "when?", use 什么时候 (what time) or a more precise question word, in the same position:

```dialogue
title: Making plans
小红: 你什么时候去北京？ | When are you going to Beijing?
马克: 我星期五去。 | I'm going on Friday.
小红: 几点？ | What time?
马克: 上午十点。 | 10 in the morning.
小红: 今天几号？ | What's the date today?
马克: 今天三号，星期三。 | It's the 3rd, Wednesday.
```

```words
时候 | time, moment
什么时候 | when
时间 | time (an amount of)
小时 | hour (duration)
分钟 | minute (duration)
前 | before; ago
后 | after
```

:::grammar[点 and 小时: telling the time vs. how long]
点 is a point on the clock; 小时 is an hour of duration. 三点 is three o'clock, 三个小时 is three hours. Likewise 五分 (five past, on the clock) versus 五分钟 (five minutes, how long).
:::

## A day in the life

```words
起床 | to get up
睡觉 | to go to bed, to sleep
睡 | to sleep
上班 | to go to work
下班 | to finish work
上学 | to go to school
上课 | to start class, to have class
下课 | to finish class
早 | early; morning
晚 | late
一半 | half (of something)
```

上 and 下 again: 上班 is going up to work, 下班 is coming down from it. Same with 上课/下课 (class).

```story
title: 安娜的星期一
zh: 安娜的星期一
paragraphs:
  - zh: 星期一早上，安娜七点起床。她七点半吃早饭，八点去上班。
    en: On Monday morning, Anna gets up at seven. She has breakfast at half past seven and goes to work at eight.
  - zh: 她上午工作四个小时。中午十二点，她和同学吃午饭。
    en: She works four hours in the morning. At noon she has lunch with a classmate.
  - zh: 下午六点她下班。晚上她学习汉语，十一点睡觉。
    en: She finishes work at six in the afternoon. In the evening she studies Chinese, and goes to bed at eleven.
questions:
  - prompt: What time does Anna get up?
    options: ['6:00', '7:00', '7:30', '8:00']
    answer: 1
  - prompt: How long does she work in the morning?
    options: [two hours, four hours, eight hours]
    answer: 1
  - prompt: What does she do in the evening?
    options: [She works., She studies Chinese., She has lunch.]
    answer: 1
```

```scene
title: Booking a dinner
setting: You call a restaurant to book a table.
partner: 服务员 (waiter)
turns:
  - they: 喂，你好！
    theyEn: Hello?
    options:
      - zh: 你好！我想明天晚上吃饭。
        en: Hello! I'd like to eat tomorrow evening.
        ok: true
      - zh: 我明天晚上吃饭吗？
        en: Do I eat tomorrow evening?
        reply: 嗯……我不知道。
        replyEn: Um… I don't know.
  - they: 好的。几点？几个人？
    theyEn: Sure. What time? How many people?
    options:
      - zh: 七点半，三个人。
        en: Half past seven, three people.
        ok: true
      - zh: 三点七个半人。
        en: Three o'clock, seven and a half people.
        reply: 七个半人？
        replyEn: Seven and a half people?
  - they: 明天是十号，星期五，晚上七点半，三个人。对吗？
    theyEn: Tomorrow is the 10th, Friday, 7:30 pm, three people. Right?
    options:
      - zh: 对，谢谢！
        en: Yes, thanks!
        ok: true
      - zh: 不客气。
        en: You're welcome.
        reply: 啊……不客气？好，明天见！
        replyEn: Er… you're welcome? OK, see you tomorrow!
end: Table booked. 明天见！
```

```compose
  - 我每天早上七点起床。

```

:::key
- Time: hour + 点 (+ minutes 分); 半 for half past; 两点 for 2:00.
- Days: 星期一 to 星期六, plus 星期天/日. Months: 一月 to 十二月. Dates: 号.
- Big before small: year → month → day → weekday → part of day → time.
- Time words go before the verb.
- 点 is a point in time; 小时 is a duration.
:::

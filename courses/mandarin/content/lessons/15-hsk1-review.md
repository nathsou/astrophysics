You have covered the core of HSK 1: about 300 words, the basic sentence patterns, and enough to introduce yourself, eat, shop, get around and chat. This lesson ties the loose ends, adds two small but mighty particles, and then puts everything to work in a story, a scene and a review.

If you have been finishing lessons, your review deck has been filling up. Ten minutes of reviews a day will do more for your Chinese than anything else in this course.

## Two particles: 吧 and 了

Sentence-final particles change the mood of a sentence, the way tone of voice does in English.

**吧 (ba)** softens a sentence into a suggestion or a guess:

- 我们走吧！ Let's go!
- 你是马克吧？ You're Mark, right?
- 好吧。 OK then. (a little reluctant)

**了 (le)** at the end of a sentence marks a **change**: something is now the case that wasn't before.

- 下雨了。 It's raining (now; it wasn't before).
- 我饿了。 I'm hungry (now).
- 他二十岁了。 He's twenty now.
- 我不去了。 I'm not going any more (I've changed my mind).

You'll meet a second use of 了, after a verb for completed actions, in lesson 16.

```words
吧 | let's…; …right?; OK then
了 | (marks a change: now…)
大家 | everyone
们 | plural suffix for people
它 | it
它们 | they (things, animals)
哪些 | which (ones)
见 | to see, to meet
男朋友 | boyfriend
女朋友 | girlfriend
```

们 only goes on people: 我们, 老师们, 同学们. Things don't take a plural at all: 三本书 is "three book", and context does the rest.

## A story: 小猫回家

Read it at your own pace. Tap any word you don't recognise, and only open the English if you're stuck: guessing from context is exactly the skill that makes reading take off.

```story
title: 小猫回家
zh: 小猫回家
paragraphs:
  - zh: 安娜住在北京。她有一只小猫，它叫“米饭”。米饭很小，非常好看。
    en: Anna lives in Beijing. She has a kitten called "Rice". Rice is very small and very cute.
  - zh: 星期六早上，安娜起床了。她叫：“米饭！米饭！”可是米饭不在家。
    en: On Saturday morning, Anna got up. She called, "Rice! Rice!" But Rice wasn't at home.
  - zh: 安娜很不高兴。她去外边找米饭。她问超市的老板：“你看见我的猫了吗？”老板说：“没看见。”
    en: Anna was very upset. She went outside to look for Rice. She asked the supermarket owner, "Have you seen my cat?" The owner said, "No, I haven't."
  - zh: 她去书店，去饭店，去学校，都没有米饭。下午，天气很冷，下雨了。安娜很累，她回家了。
    en: She went to the bookshop, the restaurant, the school; Rice wasn't anywhere. In the afternoon it got cold and started to rain. Anna was tired, and went home.
  - zh: 她开门，你们知道她看见了什么吗？米饭在她的椅子上睡觉！
    en: She opened the door, and do you know what she saw? Rice was asleep on her chair!
  - zh: 安娜说：“米饭，你在家！太好了！”米饭说：“喵。”
    en: Anna said, "Rice, you're home! Wonderful!" Rice said, "Miaow."
questions:
  - prompt: What is the cat called?
    options: [Rice, Noodles, Dumpling]
    answer: 0
  - prompt: Who did Anna ask first?
    options: [A teacher, The supermarket owner, A doctor]
    answer: 1
  - prompt: Why did Anna go home?
    options: [She found the cat., It rained and she was tired., The shops closed.]
    answer: 1
  - prompt: Where was the cat?
    options: [At school, Under the table, Asleep on Anna's chair]
    answer: 2
```

## Review: Part 3 in one sitting

Each of these drills reaches back across the last nine lessons. Do them without looking back; whatever you miss is a good sign of what to review.

```choose
title: Grammar check
items:
  - prompt: Which sentence is correct?
    options: [我是很忙。, 我很忙。, 我忙是。]
    answer: 1
    explain: Adjectives take no 是 (lesson 14).
  - prompt: How do you say "I don't have a car"?
    options: [我不有车。, 我没有车。, 我是没车。]
    answer: 1
    explain: The negative of 有 is always 没有 (lesson 8).
  - prompt: Where does "tomorrow" go?
    options: [我去北京明天。, 我明天去北京。]
    answer: 1
    explain: Time before the verb (lesson 9).
  - prompt: "\"Three books\" is…"
    options: [三书, 三个书, 三本书]
    answer: 2
    explain: Number + measure word + noun; books take 本 (lessons 8 and 11).
  - prompt: "\"She can speak Chinese\" (she learned it) is…"
    options: [她会说汉语。, 她可以说汉语。, 她能是汉语。]
    answer: 0
    explain: 会 for learned skills (lesson 13).
  - prompt: How do you ask a child their age?
    options: [你多大？, 你几岁？, 您多大年纪？]
    answer: 1
```

```scene
title: Meeting a friend's family
setting: 小红 has invited you to her parents' home for dinner. Her mother opens the door.
partner: 小红的妈妈
turns:
  - they: 你好！你是马克吧？
    theyEn: Hello! You must be Mark?
    options:
      - zh: 阿姨好！我是马克。
        en: Hello, auntie! I'm Mark.
        ok: true
      - zh: 是吧。
        en: I guess so.
        reply: 哈哈，你不知道你是谁吗？
        replyEn: Haha, you don't know who you are?
  - they: 请进，请坐！你想喝什么？
    theyEn: Come in, sit down! What would you like to drink?
    options:
      - zh: 我喝茶，谢谢。
        en: Tea, thanks.
        ok: true
      - zh: 我是茶。
        en: I am tea.
        reply: ……小红，你的朋友是茶？
        replyEn: …Xiaohong, your friend is tea?
  - they: 你在北京做什么工作？
    theyEn: What do you do in Beijing?
    options:
      - zh: 我是老师，我在一个大学工作。
        en: I'm a teacher, I work at a university.
        ok: true
      - zh: 我在北京坐。
        en: I sit in Beijing.
        reply: 坐？你工作吗？
        replyEn: Sit? Do you work?
  - they: 饭好了！大家来吃饭吧！
    theyEn: Dinner's ready! Everyone, come and eat!
    options:
      - zh: 太好了！我很饿了。
        en: Great! I'm really hungry.
        ok: true
      - zh: 不要了，谢谢。
        en: No more, thanks.
        reply: 什么？你还没吃呢！
        replyEn: What? You haven't eaten anything yet!
  - they: 好吃吗？
    theyEn: Is it good?
    options:
      - zh: 非常好吃！阿姨，你做饭做得真好！
        en: Delicious! Auntie, you're a great cook!
        ok: true
      - zh: 有点儿好吃。
        en: A bit tasty. (sounds like a complaint)
        reply: 有点儿？……好吧。
        replyEn: A bit? …I see.
end: You leave three hours later, very full, with a bag of dumplings for tomorrow.
```

:::culture
Friends' parents are addressed as 阿姨 (āyí, auntie) and 叔叔 (shūshu, uncle), whether or not you're related. You'll hear children call any adult woman 阿姨 too. And notice the 有点儿 trap in the last turn: since it suggests something unwelcome, "a bit tasty" sounds like a complaint.
:::

:::key
- 吧 makes a suggestion or a guess; sentence-final 了 marks a change.
- 们 only for people; things don't take a plural.
- The big patterns of Part 3: 是 for nouns, adjectives without 是, 有/没有, measure words, time and place before the verb, question words where the answer goes.
:::

That is HSK 1. Part 4 takes you through HSK 2: the past, comparisons, plans and reasons.

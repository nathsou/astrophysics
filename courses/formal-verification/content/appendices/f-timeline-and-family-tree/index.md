Formal verification has a long history in which ideas often arrived decades before the tools that made them practical. Each part of the course opens with an essay, *How we got here*, on the history of its own family of techniques. This appendix collects that history in two views.

## The timeline

One lane per family of techniques, plus the industrial adoptions and the failures that paid for much of the work. Select a dot to jump to its event. Each event links to the chapter where it matters.

::timeline

## The family tree of tools

Tools are built on other tools and on other tools’ ideas. A solid arrow means *descends from*: the newer tool is built on, or is a direct successor of, the older one. A dashed arrow means *influenced*: an idea or technique carried over. Select a node to light up its lineage.

::family-tree

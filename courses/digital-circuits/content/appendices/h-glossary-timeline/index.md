This appendix is for looking things up. The **glossary** lists every term the course defines, with the chapter that introduces it. (In the chapters, the same terms are underlined: hover over one for its definition.) The **timeline** puts the history cards of the whole course in one line, from Volta’s pile of 1800 to the open process kits of 2020. The **bibliography** lists every source that a chapter cites, so that any date, number or quotation in the course can be checked.

## Glossary

Type in the box to filter by word or by definition, or use the letters to jump. Each entry ends with a link to the chapter where the term is introduced.

::glossary-index

## Timeline

The history cards of the chapters are collected here in order of date. A link after the title leads to the chapter that tells the story. Type to filter by year, person or word.

::timeline

## Bibliography

Sources are grouped by the part of the course that first cites them, and each entry lists the chapters that cite it. Wherever possible the link goes to the original: the paper, the patent, the datasheet or the museum’s page. Books have no link. Where a source is a general reference such as an encyclopaedia entry, the note says what the chapter takes from it and nothing more.

::bibliography-index

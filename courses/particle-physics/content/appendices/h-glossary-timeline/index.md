This appendix is for looking things up. The **glossary** lists every term the course defines, with the chapter that introduces it; in the chapters the same terms are underlined, and hovering over one shows its definition. The **timeline** puts the cards of the chapters' history boxes in one line, in order of date, so that the course's story can be read straight through. The **bibliography** lists every source that a chapter cites, with a link to the original where there is one.

## Timeline

The history cards of the chapters, collected and sorted by year. The title of each is followed by the people involved and the chapter that tells the story.

::timeline

## Bibliography

The sources, in alphabetical order of the first author. Primary sources (the paper, the lecture, the data release) are preferred wherever a chapter states a date or a number. Books have no link. Where a source is a general reference such as the Particle Data Group's review, the note says what the course takes from it.

::all-references

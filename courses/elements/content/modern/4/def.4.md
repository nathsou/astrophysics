---
title: Figure circumscribed about a circle
---
A polygon is **circumscribed** about a circle when each of its sides touches the circle, that is, each side is tangent to it ([[3.def.2]]). Modern texts call such a polygon *tangential*. Every triangle is tangential ([[4.4]]); a quadrilateral is tangential only when the two sums of its opposite sides are equal, a fact Euclid does not state.

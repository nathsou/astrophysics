---
title: Circumscribing a triangle of given shape
---
**Statement.** About a given circle, circumscribe a triangle with the same angles as a given triangle @triangle(DEF).

**Construction.** Produce @EF both ways to @G and @H. From the centre @K draw any radius @KB, and make $\angle BKA = \angle DEG$ and $\angle BKC = \angle DFH$ on either side of it ([[1.23]]). The tangents at @A, @B and @C ([[3.16]]) form the triangle @triangle(LMN).

**Proof.** A tangent is perpendicular to the radius at its point of contact ([[3.18]]), so the quadrilateral $AMBK$ has right angles at @A and @B. Its angles add up to four right angles, so
$$\angle AMB = 180° - \angle AKB = 180° - \angle DEG = \angle DEF,$$
the last step because $DEG$ and $DEF$ are supplementary ([[1.13]]). In the same way $\angle LNB = \angle DFE$, and the third angles are equal by [[1.32]].

The construction works because the central angles $\angle AKB$, $\angle BKC$, $\angle CKA$ are the exterior angles of @triangle(DEF), which add up to $360°$.

**In the figure.** Drag the vertices of @triangle(DEF), and drag @B around the circle to turn the triangle @triangle(LMN).

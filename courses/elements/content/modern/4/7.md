---
title: The square circumscribed about a circle
---
**Statement.** About a given circle, circumscribe a square.

**Construction.** Draw two perpendicular diameters @AC and @BD and the tangents at their four ends ([[3.16]]). They meet at @F, @G, @H, @K.

**Proof.** Each tangent is perpendicular to its radius ([[3.18]]), so each is parallel to the other diameter ([[1.28]]): @GH and @FK are parallel to @AC, and @GF and @HK to @BD. The figure is cut into parallelograms, so $GH = FK = AC$ and $GF = HK = BD$ ([[1.34]]). The diameters are equal, so $FGHK$ is equilateral. Its angles equal the right angle $AEB$ at the centre (opposite angles of the parallelogram $GBEA$), so it is a square.

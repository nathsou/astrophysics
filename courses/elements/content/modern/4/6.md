---
title: The square inscribed in a circle
---
**Statement.** In a given circle, inscribe a square.

**Construction.** Draw two diameters @AC and @BD at right angles, and join their ends.

**Proof.** The four triangles at the centre @E have two sides equal (radii) and a right angle between them, so their bases are equal (SAS, [[1.4]]): the quadrilateral is equilateral. Each of its angles stands on a diameter, so it is right ([[3.31]]). An equilateral right-angled quadrilateral is a square ([[1.def.22]]).

**In the figure.** Drag @E or @A.

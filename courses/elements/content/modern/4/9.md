---
title: The circle circumscribed about a square
---
**Statement.** About a given square, circumscribe a circle.

**Construction.** Join the diagonals @AC and @BD, meeting at @E.

**Proof.** The diagonal @AC bisects the angle at @A, by SSS ([[1.8]]) on the triangles $DAC$ and $BAC$; likewise for the other diagonal. So the angles $EAB$ and $EBA$ are both half a right angle, and $EA = EB$ ([[1.6]]). In the same way $EA = EB = EC = ED$, and the circle with centre @E through @A passes through all four vertices.

**In the figure.** Drag @A or @B.

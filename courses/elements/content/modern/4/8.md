---
title: The circle inscribed in a square
---
**Statement.** In a given square, inscribe a circle.

**Construction.** Bisect @AD at @E and @AB at @F, and draw @EH parallel to @AB and @FK parallel to @AD. They meet at @G, the centre.

**Proof.** The two lines cut the square into four equal squares, so $GE = GF = GH = GK$, each being half a side ([[1.34]]). The circle with centre @G through @E passes through the other three points, and it touches the sides there, because each side is perpendicular to the radius drawn to it ([[3.16]]).

**In the figure.** Drag @A or @B.

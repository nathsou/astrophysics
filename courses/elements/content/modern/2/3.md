title: Rectangle on a whole and a part
---
**Statement.** If $AB$ is cut at $C$, then
$$AB \cdot BC = AC \cdot CB + CB^2.$$
With $AC = a$ and $CB = b$: $(a+b)b = ab + b^2$.

**Proof.** Draw the square @figure(CE) on @CB and complete the rectangle @figure(AE) with height $CB$. The line @CD cuts it into the rectangle @figure(AD), with sides $AC$ and $DC = CB$, and the square itself. Again this is [[2.1]], now with the uncut line equal to one of the parts.

**In the figure.** Drag @C along @AB. When @C approaches @A, the rectangle @figure(AD) vanishes and the rectangle becomes the square.

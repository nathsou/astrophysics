---
title: Angle of a segment
---
The **angle of a segment** is the angle between the chord and the arc at an end of the chord.

This is a *mixed* angle, with one straight side and one curved side. Such angles appear in only a few places in the Elements, notably [[3.16]] (the angle between a diameter and the circle, and the "horn angle" between a tangent and the circle) and the end of [[3.31]]. Modern geometry measures the angle between curves by their tangent lines, which makes the horn angle zero; Euclid's mixed angles do not fit that scheme, and [[3.16]] shows why.

---
title: Equal arcs have equal chords
---
**Statement.** In equal circles, equal arcs $BGC$ and $EHF$ are subtended by equal chords $BC = EF$.

**Proof.** With centres @K and @L, equal arcs stand on equal central angles, $\angle BKC = \angle ELF$ ([[3.27]]). The triangles $BKC$ and $ELF$ have two sides equal (radii) and the included angles equal, so $BC = EF$ by SAS ([[1.4]]).

This is the converse of [[3.28]]. Together they say that, in a fixed circle, the chord length $2r\sin\frac{\theta}{2}$ is a one-to-one function of the arc's central angle $\theta$ as long as the arcs compared are both less, or both greater, than a semicircle.

---
title: Angle standing on an arc
---
When the two lines of an angle cut off an arc of a circle, the angle **stands on** that arc (it subtends it).

An inscribed angle $\angle APB$ stands on the arc $AB$ that does not contain $P$. A central angle $\angle AOB$ stands on the arc $AB$ between its arms. [[3.20]] relates the two when they stand on the same arc.

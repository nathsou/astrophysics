---
title: Sector
---
A **sector** is the region bounded by two radii and the arc between them: a "slice" of the circle.

Its angle is the central angle, and its area is proportional to that angle. Euclid does not measure the area of a sector, since he never gives the area of a circle as a number; [[12.2]] only proves that circles are to one another as the squares on their diameters. [[6.33]] later shows that, in equal circles, angles at the centre are proportional to the arcs they stand on.

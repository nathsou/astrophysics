---
title: Similar segments on equal bases are equal
---
**Statement.** If the segments $AEB$ and $CFD$ are similar and stand on equal chords $AB = CD$, they are equal (congruent).

**Proof (by superposition).** Place @A on @C and the line @AB along @CD. Since $AB = CD$, @B falls on @D. The segment $AEB$ must then coincide with $CFD$. Otherwise it falls inside or outside it, contradicting [[3.23]], or it falls "awry" like @CGD and crosses it, and then two circles meet in more than two points, contradicting [[3.10]].

In modern terms: a segment is determined by its chord and its angle. The radius is $r = \frac{AB}{2\sin\theta}$ where $\theta$ is the angle in the segment, and the arc bulges by $\tfrac{AB}{2}\cot\tfrac{\theta}{2}$. Equal chord and equal angle give equal $r$ and equal bulge.

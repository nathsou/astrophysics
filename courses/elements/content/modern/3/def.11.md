---
title: Similar segments
---
Two segments of circles are **similar** when they admit equal angles, that is, when the angles in them are equal.

By [[3.21]] this is well defined, and by [[3.20]] it amounts to saying that the arcs have equal central angles. In modern terms two segments are similar exactly when one is a scaled copy of the other. [[3.23]] and [[3.24]] prove the first facts about them: on a given base there is only one similar segment on a given side, and on equal bases similar segments are equal.

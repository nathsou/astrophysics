---
title: Chord at a greater distance
---
A chord is **at a greater distance** from the centre when the perpendicular from the centre to it is greater.

Together with the previous definition, this orders chords by their distance $d$ from the centre. [[3.15]] shows that the order is reversed for length: a chord at distance $d$ has length $2\sqrt{r^2 - d^2}$, which decreases as $d$ grows, and the diameter ($d = 0$) is the longest.

---
title: Chords equally distant from the centre
---
Two chords of a circle are **equally distant from the centre** when the perpendiculars dropped on them from the centre are equal.

This is simply the modern distance from a point to a line: the length of the perpendicular. Euclid needs a definition because "distance" is not yet a word he has for a point and a line. [[3.14]] shows that equal chords are exactly the chords at equal distance.

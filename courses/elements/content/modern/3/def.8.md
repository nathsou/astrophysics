---
title: Angle in a segment
---
The **angle in a segment** is the angle $\angle APB$ at a point $P$ of the arc, where $AB$ is the chord that bounds the segment. Today this is the *inscribed angle*.

The definition quietly assumes that the angle does not depend on the choice of $P$. That is [[3.21]]: all angles in the same segment are equal. Until then, "the angle in a segment" is a phrase waiting for a theorem.

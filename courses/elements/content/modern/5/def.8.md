---
title: Least proportion
---
**Definition.** A proportion needs at least three terms: $a : b = b : c$, with the middle term used twice.

Such a proportion is called *continued*, and $b$ is the *mean proportional* between $a$ and $c$; in modern terms $b = \sqrt{ac}$, the geometric mean. [[6.13]] constructs it for straight lines. With only two terms there is nothing to compare: a proportion is a relation between two ratios.

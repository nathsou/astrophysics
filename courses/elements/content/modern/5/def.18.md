---
title: Perturbed proportion
---
**Definition.** Three magnitudes $a, b, c$ and three others $d, e, f$ are in *perturbed* proportion when
$$a : b = e : f \qquad\text{and}\qquad b : c = d : e,$$
that is, the ratios match crosswise instead of in order.

The conclusion is still $a : c = d : f$ ([[5.23]]). For fractions, $\frac ac = \frac ab \cdot \frac bc = \frac ef \cdot \frac de = \frac df$: the same product taken in the other order. So [[5.23]] is, in effect, the commutativity of the product of ratios.

---
title: Part (submultiple)
---
**Definition.** A magnitude $a$ is a *part* of a larger magnitude $b$ of the same kind when $a$ measures $b$: $b = n a$ for some whole number $n \ge 2$. In modern terms, $a$ is an aliquot part, $a = b/n$.

"Part" here always means an exact submultiple. Three is a part of twelve; eight is not a part of twelve, although it is smaller. The same word is used for numbers in [[7.def.3]]. Book V defines it for magnitudes because the propositions that follow are all about multiples and parts.

---
title: Triplicate ratio
---
**Definition.** If $a : b = b : c = c : d$, then $a : d$ is the *triplicate* of the ratio $a : b$, and so on for longer chains.

With $r = a/b$ this is $a/d = r^3$. Volumes scale as the cube of lengths, and Euclid says so in this language: similar solids are in the triplicate ratio of their corresponding sides, and spheres are in the triplicate ratio of their diameters ([[12.18]]).

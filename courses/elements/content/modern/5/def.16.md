---
title: Conversion of a ratio (convertendo)
---
**Definition.** For $a > b$, the *conversion* of $a : b$ is the ratio $a : (a - b)$. From $a : b = c : d$ one passes *convertendo* to $a : (a - b) = c : (c - d)$.

It follows from separation and inversion, and it is the porism of [[5.19]]. For fractions: if $a/b = c/d = r$, both sides equal $r/(r - 1)$.

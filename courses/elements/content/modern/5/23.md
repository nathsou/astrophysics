---
title: Perturbed ex aequali
---
**Statement.** If $a : b = e : f$ and $b : c = d : e$, then
$$a : c = d : f.$$
In fractions, $\frac ac = \frac ab \cdot \frac bc = \frac ef \cdot \frac de$, while $\frac df = \frac de \cdot \frac ef$. The two products have the same factors in the opposite order, so the proposition is the commutativity of the product of ratios.

**Proof.** Take @G, @H, @K $= mA, mB, mD$ and @L, @M, @N $= nC, nE, nF$. Then:
- $G : H = A : B = E : F = M : N$, by [[5.15]] and [[5.11]];
- $B : D = C : E$ by alternating $B : C = D : E$ ([[5.16]]); so $H : K = C : E = L : M$ ([[5.15]]), and alternating again, $H : L = K : M$.

So $G, H, L$ and $K, M, N$ are in perturbed proportion: $G : H = M : N$ and $H : L = K : M$. By [[5.21]], $G \gtrless L$ exactly when $K \gtrless N$, and that is [[5.def.5]] for $A : C = D : F$.

The proof alternates twice, so all six magnitudes must be of one kind. The figure shows the equalities $G : H = M : N$ and $H : L = K : M$ and the final comparison.

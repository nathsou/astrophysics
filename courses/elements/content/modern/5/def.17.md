---
title: Ratio ex aequali
---
**Definition.** Given magnitudes $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_k$ and $b_1, b_2, \dots, b_k$ with
$$a_1 : a_2 = b_1 : b_2,\quad a_2 : a_3 = b_2 : b_3,\quad \dots,\quad a_{k-1} : a_k = b_{k-1} : b_k,$$
taking the ratio *ex aequali* ("from equality in the number of terms") means concluding $a_1 : a_k = b_1 : b_k$: the intermediate terms are dropped.

For fractions this is the telescoping product $\frac{a_1}{a_k} = \frac{a_1}{a_2}\cdot\frac{a_2}{a_3}\cdots\frac{a_{k-1}}{a_k}$, so it says that the product of ratios is well defined. It is proved in [[5.22]].

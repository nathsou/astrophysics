---
title: Corresponding magnitudes
---
**Definition.** In a proportion $a : b = c : d$, the *antecedents* $a$ and $c$ correspond to each other, and so do the *consequents* $b$ and $d$.

The antecedent is the first term of a ratio and the consequent the second, like numerator and denominator. Euclid needs the word because many of his statements say "taken in corresponding order": equimultiples of $a$ and $c$, other equimultiples of $b$ and $d$.

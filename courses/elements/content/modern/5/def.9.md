---
title: Duplicate ratio
---
**Definition.** If $a : b = b : c$, then $a : c$ is the *duplicate* of the ratio $a : b$.

In modern terms, if $a/b = b/c = r$ then $a/c = r^2$: the duplicate ratio is the square of the ratio. "Duplicate" means compounded with itself, not doubled. The main use is [[6.19]]: similar triangles are to one another in the duplicate ratio of corresponding sides, that is, areas scale as the square of lengths. [[12.2]] states the same for circles and their diameters.

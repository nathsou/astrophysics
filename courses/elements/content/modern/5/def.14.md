---
title: Composition of a ratio (componendo)
---
**Definition.** The *composition* of $a : b$ is the ratio $(a + b) : b$. From $a : b = c : d$ one passes *componendo* to $(a + b) : b = (c + d) : d$.

In modern terms, $\frac ab + 1 = \frac{a + b}b$. It is proved in [[5.18]]. Do not confuse it with the *compound* ratio of Book VI ([[6.23]]), which is a product of ratios.

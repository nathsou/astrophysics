---
title: Parts and their multiples
---
**Statement.** For any whole number $m$,
$$c : f = mc : mf.$$
In the figure, @AB $= m\,C$ and @DE $= m\,F$ (Heath draws $m = 3$).

**Proof.** Cut @AB into the copies @AG, @GH, @HB of @C and @DE into the copies @DK, @KL, @LE of @F. Each copy of $C$ has to its partner the ratio $C : F$ ([[5.7]]), so $AG : DK = GH : KL = HB : LE$. By [[5.12]], the sum of the antecedents is to the sum of the consequents in the same ratio: $AG : DK = AB : DE$. That is, $C : F = AB : DE$.

This is the cancellation law $\frac{mc}{mf} = \frac cf$. Together with [[5.4]] it lets Euclid replace any ratio by a ratio of multiples, which is how [[5.16]] gets around the fact that it cannot multiply a ratio by anything.

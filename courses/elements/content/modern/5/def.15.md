---
title: Separation of a ratio (separando)
---
**Definition.** For $a > b$, the *separation* of $a : b$ is the ratio $(a - b) : b$. From $a : b = c : d$ one passes *separando* to $(a - b) : b = (c - d) : d$.

In modern terms, $\frac ab - 1 = \frac{a - b}b$. It is proved in [[5.17]], where Euclid states it the other way round: from $(a + b) : b = (c + d) : d$ to $a : b = c : d$.

---
title: Pyramid
---
A pyramid is a solid bounded by planes, formed by joining a plane base (a polygon) to a point outside its plane.

Its faces are the base and one triangle for each side of the base. A pyramid on a triangular base is a tetrahedron, and if all four faces are equilateral it is the regular tetrahedron of [[13.13]]. The volume of a pyramid, one third of base times height, is proved in Book XII ([[12.7]]) by the method of exhaustion.

---
title: A similar parallelepiped on a given line
---
**Statement.** On a given line @AB, construct a parallelepiped similar and similarly situated to a given parallelepiped @solid(CD).

**Construction (Euclid's).** At @A construct a solid angle equal to the one at @C ([[11.26]]), so that $\angle BAH = \angle ECF$, $\angle BAK = \angle ECG$ and $\angle KAH = \angle GCF$. Choose the other two edges so that
$$EC : CG = BA : AK, \qquad GC : CF = KA : AH$$
([[6.12]]); then also $EC : CF = BA : AH$ ([[5.22]]). Complete the solid $AL$. Its three faces at @A are similar to the three faces at @C (equal angles, proportional sides, [[6.def.1]]), and opposite faces are equal and similar ([[11.24]]), so the solids are similar ([[11.def.9]]).

:::code A scaling
The new solid is the old one moved rigidly and scaled by $k = AB : CE$. Its volume is $k^3$ times the old one, as [[11.33]] will prove. The figure shows the ratio of volumes; compare it with $(AB : CE)^3$.
:::

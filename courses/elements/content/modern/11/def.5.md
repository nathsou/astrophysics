---
title: Inclination of a line to a plane
---
Let a line meet a plane at $A$ and let $B$ be another point of it. Drop the perpendicular from $B$ to the plane, with foot $C$ ([[11.11]]). The inclination of the line to the plane is the angle $BAC$ between the line and its projection $AC$.

In vectors: if the line has direction $u$ and the plane has unit normal $n$, the inclination $\theta$ satisfies $\sin\theta = |u \cdot n| / |u|$. It is the smallest angle the line makes with any line of the plane through $A$, which is why it is the natural measure. [[11.35]] is about equal inclinations.

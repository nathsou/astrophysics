---
title: Parallel planes
---
Parallel planes are planes that do not meet.

This is the analogue of parallel lines in the plane ([[1.def.23]]), with one difference: two lines in space that do not meet need not be parallel (they may be *skew*), but two planes that do not meet always are. With equations $n_1\cdot X = c_1$ and $n_2 \cdot X = c_2$, the planes are parallel exactly when $n_1$ and $n_2$ are proportional and the planes are distinct. [[11.14]], [[11.15]] and [[11.16]] give Euclid's criteria.

---
title: Icosahedron
---
An icosahedron is a solid bounded by twenty equal equilateral triangles, five at each vertex. It is constructed in [[13.16]].

Five angles of $60^\circ$ make $300^\circ$ at each vertex. Six would make $360^\circ$ and lie flat, which is why no regular solid has six triangles at a vertex ([[13.18]]).

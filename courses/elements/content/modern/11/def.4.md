---
title: Perpendicular planes
---
Two planes are perpendicular when every line drawn in one of them at right angles to their common section is perpendicular to the other plane.

With normals $n_1$, $n_2$, the planes are perpendicular exactly when $n_1 \cdot n_2 = 0$. Euclid's version reduces the relation between planes to the relation between a line and a plane ([[11.def.3]]). [[11.18]] and [[11.19]] are the two basic facts about it.

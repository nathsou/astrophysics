---
title: Similar cones and cylinders
---
Cones (or cylinders) are similar when their axes are in the same ratio as the diameters of their bases: $h_1 : h_2 = d_1 : d_2$.

For solids of revolution this definition is sound (compare [[11.def.9]]): the solid is determined by the turning triangle or rectangle, and proportional sides make these similar figures. [[12.12]] proves that similar cones and cylinders are to one another in the triplicate ratio (the cube) of the diameters of their bases.

---
title: Proportional lines, proportional similar solids
---
**Statement.** Four lines are proportional, $AB : CD = EF : GH$, exactly when similar and similarly described parallelepipeds on them are proportional: $KA : LC = ME : NG$.

**Proof (Euclid's).** By [[11.33]], $KA : LC$ is the triplicate of $AB : CD$ and $ME : NG$ is the triplicate of $EF : GH$. Equal ratios have equal triplicates, and conversely.

The converse is the less obvious half. It needs "if the cubes of two ratios are equal, the ratios are equal". Euclid treats it as evident; with real numbers it holds because $x \mapsto x^3$ is strictly increasing on positive numbers. The same step for duplicate ratios is [[6.22]].

The figure makes $GH = CD \cdot EF / AB$ and checks $KA : LC = ME : NG$, and that this ratio is $(AB : CD)^3$.
:::

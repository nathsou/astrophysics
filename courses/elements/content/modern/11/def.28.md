---
title: Dodecahedron
---
A dodecahedron is a solid bounded by twelve equal regular pentagons, three at each vertex. It is constructed in [[13.17]].

Three angles of $108^\circ$ make $324^\circ$ at each vertex, less than four right angles ([[11.21]]). Four would make $432^\circ$, which is impossible.

Here Euclid writes "equilateral and equiangular" pentagons, because an equilateral pentagon need not be regular. For the triangles of [[11.def.26]] and [[11.def.27]] the word "equilateral" was enough.

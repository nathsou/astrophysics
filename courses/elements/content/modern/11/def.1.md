---
title: Solid
---
A solid is a figure with three dimensions: length, breadth and depth.

Like the definition of a point ([[1.def.1]]) and of a surface ([[1.def.5]]), this one describes rather than defines. It completes the sequence point, line, surface, solid, each with one more dimension than the last. In modern terms a solid is a bounded region of space $\mathbb{R}^3$ with non-empty interior, and its boundary is a surface ([[11.def.2]]).

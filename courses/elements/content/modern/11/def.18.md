---
title: Cone
---
A cone is the solid swept out when a right-angled triangle turns about one of the sides containing the right angle.

The fixed side is the axis and becomes the height; the other side sweeps out the circular base. The cone is *right-angled*, *obtuse-angled* or *acute-angled* according to the angle at the apex, which depends on whether the fixed side equals, is less than, or is greater than the turning side.

This is a *right circular* cone. The three kinds matter historically: before Apollonius, the conic sections were obtained by cutting each kind of cone with a plane perpendicular to one of its generators, and the parabola was called "the section of a right-angled cone".

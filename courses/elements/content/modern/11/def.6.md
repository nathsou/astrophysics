---
title: Dihedral angle
---
The inclination of one plane to another is the angle between two lines drawn at the same point of their common section, one in each plane, both at right angles to the section. Today it is called the *dihedral angle*.

The angle does not depend on the point chosen on the section: moving the point translates both lines, and translations preserve angles ([[11.10]]). With unit normals $n_1$, $n_2$, the dihedral angle $\varphi$ (taken acute, as Euclid does) satisfies $\cos\varphi = |n_1 \cdot n_2|$.

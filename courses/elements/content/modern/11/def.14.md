---
title: Sphere
---
A sphere is the solid swept out when a semicircle turns about its diameter until it returns to its starting position.

This is a definition by motion, unlike the circle, which Euclid defined by a property of distances ([[1.def.15]]). The equivalent modern definition is the ball $\{X : |X - O| \le r\}$, with boundary the surface $|X - O| = r$. That every point of the swept surface is at distance $r$ from the centre is immediate, since each point lies on one of the positions of the semicircle.

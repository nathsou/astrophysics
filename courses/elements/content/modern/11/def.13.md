---
title: Prism
---
A prism is a solid bounded by two equal, similar and parallel faces (the ends), with all the other faces parallelograms.

The two ends are translates of each other. The side edges are parallel and equal, but they need not be perpendicular to the ends. A parallelepiped is a prism whose ends are parallelograms. [[11.39]] and Book XII use prisms with triangular ends, which are halves of parallelepipeds ([[11.28]]).

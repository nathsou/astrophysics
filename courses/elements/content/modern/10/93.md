---
title: The side of ρ times a third apotome
---
**Statement.** With $\rho = 1$: if $x - y$ is a third apotome ([[10.def3.3]]), then $\sqrt{x - y}$ is a second apotome of a medial straight line ([[10.75]]). Example:
$$\sqrt{3\sqrt2 - \sqrt{10}} = \frac{\sqrt5}{\sqrt[4]{2}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt[4]{2}},$$
two medial lines whose rectangle $\sqrt5/\sqrt2 = \sqrt{10}/2$ is medial too.

**Proof.** Same construction as [[10.91]]. Neither $AG$ nor $GD$ is commensurable with $\rho$, so all four rectangles @figure(AI), @figure(FK), @figure(DH), @figure(EK) are medial ([[10.21]]). Hence $LP$ and $PN$ are medial lines, commensurable in square because $AF$ and $FG$ are commensurable ([[10.17]]), and their rectangle $LP \cdot PN = EK$ is medial.

Euclid also checks that $LP$, $PN$ are incommensurable in *length*: $AF$ is commensurable with $AG$, $EG$ with $GD$, and $AG$, $GD$ are incommensurable, so $AI : EK$ is irrational, and with it $LM : MN = LP : PN$.

So @LN is a second apotome of a medial straight line. It mirrors [[10.56]].

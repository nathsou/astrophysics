---
title: A line commensurable with a minor line is minor
---
**Statement.** If $x - y$ is minor ([[10.76]]) and $\lambda$ is rational, then $\lambda x - \lambda y$ is minor.

**Proof.** With the construction of [[10.103]], $CF = \lambda x$ and $FD = \lambda y$. Scaling multiplies every area by $\lambda^2$, a rational number:

- $CF^2 : FD^2 = AE^2 : EB^2$ is irrational, so @CF, @FD are incommensurable in square;
- $CF^2 + FD^2 = \lambda^2 (AE^2 + EB^2)$ is rational (Euclid gets this by componendo, [[5.18]]);
- $CF \cdot FD = \lambda^2\, AE \cdot EB$ is medial ([[10.23]] Por.).

These are the conditions of [[10.76]], so @CD is minor. It mirrors [[10.68]].

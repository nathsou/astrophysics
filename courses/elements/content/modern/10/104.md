---
title: A line commensurable with an apotome of a medial
---
**Statement.** If $x - y$ is a first (second) apotome of a medial straight line and $\lambda$ is rational, then $\lambda x - \lambda y$ is a first (second) apotome of a medial straight line.

**Proof.** With the construction of [[10.103]], @CF and @FD are commensurable with @AE and @EB, so they are medial ([[10.23]]) and commensurable in square only ([[10.13]]): @CD is an apotome of a medial straight line. Which one depends on the rectangle. From $AE : EB = CF : FD$,
$$AE^2 : AE \cdot EB = CF^2 : CF \cdot FD,$$
and $AE^2$ is commensurable with $CF^2$, so the rectangles are commensurable. A rectangle commensurable with a rational one is rational; one commensurable with a medial one is medial ([[10.23]] Por.). So the order is kept. It mirrors [[10.67]].

---
title: Fourth binomial
---
A binomial $x + y$ is **fourth** when $\sqrt{x^2 - y^2}$ is *incommensurable* with the greater term $x$, and $x$ is commensurable with the rational line $\rho$.

Example: $2 + \sqrt2$. Here $\sqrt{4 - 2} = \sqrt2$, and $\sqrt2/2$ is irrational.

The fourth, fifth and sixth binomials are the ones whose square roots cannot be split into two lines commensurable in square ([[10.18]]); the square root of $\rho$ times a fourth binomial is a major ([[10.57]]). See the table under [[10.def2.1]].

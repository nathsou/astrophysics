---
title: Second apotome
---
An apotome $x - y$ (whole $x$, annex $y$) is **second** when $\sqrt{x^2 - y^2}$ is commensurable with the whole $x$, and the *annex* $y$ is commensurable with the rational line $\rho$.

Example: $3\sqrt2 - 4$. Here $\sqrt{18 - 16} = \sqrt2$, which is commensurable with $3\sqrt2$, and the annex $4$ is rational.

The square root of $\rho$ times a second apotome is a first apotome of a medial straight line ([[10.92]]). See the table under [[10.def3.1]].

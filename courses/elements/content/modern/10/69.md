---
title: A line commensurable with the side of a rational plus a medial area is another
---
**Statement.** If @AB is the side of a rational plus a medial area and @CD is commensurable with it, then $CD$ is also the side of a rational plus a medial area.

**Proof.** As in [[10.68]]: cut $CD$ at @F in the same ratio as @E cuts $AB$. The sum of the squares on $CF$, $FD$ and their rectangle are commensurable with those on $AE$, $EB$, so the first stays medial and the second stays rational, while $CF$, $FD$ stay incommensurable in square ([[10.40]]).

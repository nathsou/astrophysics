---
title: The side of ρ times a fourth apotome is minor
---
**Statement.** With $\rho = 1$: if $x - y$ is a fourth apotome ([[10.def3.4]]), then $\sqrt{x - y}$ is minor ([[10.76]]). Example:
$$\sqrt{2 - \sqrt2} = \sqrt{1 + \tfrac{\sqrt2}{2}} - \sqrt{1 - \tfrac{\sqrt2}{2}}.$$

**Proof.** Same construction as [[10.91]], but the apotome is fourth, so $\sqrt{x^2 - y^2}$ is incommensurable with $x$ and the rectangle $AF \cdot FG$ now cuts @AG into *incommensurable* parts ([[10.18]]). Then:

- $LP^2 + PN^2 = AF + FG = AG$ is rational, since $AG$ is commensurable with $\rho$: the whole @figure(AK) is rational;
- $2\,LP \cdot PN = GD$ is medial, since $GD$ is rational but incommensurable with $\rho$: @figure(DK) is medial;
- $LP^2 : PN^2 = AF : FG$ is irrational, so $LP$, $PN$ are incommensurable in square.

Those are exactly the conditions of [[10.76]]: @LN is minor. It mirrors [[10.57]], where the same data give a major line.

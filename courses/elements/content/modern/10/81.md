---
title: A second apotome of a medial has only one annex
---
**Statement.** To a second apotome of a medial ([[10.75]]) only one medial line can be annexed that is commensurable in square only with the whole and contains with it a medial rectangle.

**Proof.** Everything is medial, so, as in [[10.44]], Euclid applies the areas to a rational line @EF and reduces to [[10.79]]. Let @AB have annex @BC and, if possible, also @BD. Apply @figure(EG) $= AC^2 + CB^2$ with breadth @EM, and take away @figure(HG) $= 2\,AC\cdot CB$ with breadth @HM; the rest @figure(EL) is $AB^2$ ([[2.7]]). Apply @figure(EI) $= AD^2 + DB^2$ with breadth @EN; the rest @figure(HI) is $2\,AD\cdot DB$. As in [[10.75]], $EM$ and $MH$ are rational and commensurable in square only, so @EH is an apotome with annex $HM$. In the same way $HN$ is also an annex to $EH$. Two different annexes contradict [[10.79]].

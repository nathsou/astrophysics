---
title: Commensurable with the sixth subtractive line
---
**Statement.** If $x - y$ "produces with a medial area a medial whole" ([[10.78]]) and $\lambda$ is rational, then so does $\lambda x - \lambda y$.

**Proof.** As in [[10.105]]: scaling by $\lambda$ multiplies $CF^2 + FD^2$ and $CF \cdot FD$ by $\lambda^2$. Both stay medial, and their ratio does not change, so they stay incommensurable with each other. @CF, @FD remain incommensurable in square. So @CD is of the same kind as @AB.

With [[10.103]]–[[10.107]], each of the six subtractive kinds is closed under rational scaling, as [[10.66]]–[[10.70]] showed for the additive ones. The same holds for the medial line ([[10.23]]).

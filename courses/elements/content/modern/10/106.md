---
title: Commensurable with the fifth subtractive line
---
**Statement.** If $x - y$ "produces with a rational area a medial whole" ([[10.77]]) and $\lambda$ is rational, then so does $\lambda x - \lambda y$.

**Proof.** Exactly as in [[10.105]]: @CF, @FD are $\lambda$ times @AE, @EB, so they are incommensurable in square; $CF^2 + FD^2 = \lambda^2(AE^2 + EB^2)$ is medial because a rational multiple of a medial area is medial; and $CF \cdot FD = \lambda^2\, AE \cdot EB$ is rational. It mirrors [[10.69]].

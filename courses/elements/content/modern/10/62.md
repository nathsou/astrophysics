---
title: The square of a second bimedial gives a third binomial
---
**Statement.** If @AB is a second bimedial cut at @C, then $AB^2$ applied to a rational line @DE $= 1$ has as breadth @DG a third binomial. Converse of [[10.56]].

**Proof.** Same construction as [[10.60]]. Both $AC^2 + CB^2$ and $2\,AC\cdot CB$ are medial, so @DM and @MG are both rational and incommensurable in length with $DE$ ([[10.22]]). They are incommensurable with each other because $AC^2 + CB^2$ is incommensurable with $2\,AC\cdot CB$ (as $AC$ is incommensurable in length with $CB$; [[10.11]]). As before, $DK$ and $KM$ are commensurable, so $\sqrt{DM^2 - MG^2}$ is commensurable with $DM$ ([[10.17]]). Neither term is commensurable with $DE$: a third binomial.

---
title: Fifth binomial
---
A binomial $x + y$ is **fifth** when $\sqrt{x^2 - y^2}$ is incommensurable with $x$, and the lesser term $y$ is commensurable with the rational line $\rho$.

Example: $\sqrt2 + 1$. Here $\sqrt{2 - 1} = 1$, and $1/\sqrt2$ is irrational.

The square root of $\rho$ times a fifth binomial is the side of a rational plus a medial area ([[10.58]]). See the table under [[10.def2.1]].

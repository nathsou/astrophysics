---
title: An apotome has only one annex
---
**Statement.** Only one rational line can be annexed to an apotome so that it is commensurable in square only with the whole. In algebra: if $\sqrt a - \sqrt b = \sqrt c - \sqrt d$ with positive rationals $a, b, c, d$ and $\sqrt{a/b}$, $\sqrt{c/d}$ irrational, then $a = c$ and $b = d$. This is [[10.42]] for differences.

**Proof.** Let @AB be the apotome with annex @BC, so @AC, @CB are rational and commensurable in square only. Suppose @BD is another annex. By [[2.7]], both pairs exceed twice their rectangle by the same square $AB^2$:
$$AC^2 + CB^2 - 2\,AC\cdot CB = AB^2 = AD^2 + DB^2 - 2\,AD\cdot DB,$$
so
$$(AD^2 + DB^2) - (AC^2 + CB^2) = 2\,AD\cdot DB - 2\,AC\cdot CB.$$
The left side is rational; the right is a difference of two medial areas ([[10.21]]) and cannot be a nonzero rational ([[10.26]]).

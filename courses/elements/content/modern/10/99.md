---
title: The square of a second apotome of a medial, applied to ρ
---
**Statement.** With $\rho = 1$: if $x - y$ is a second apotome of a medial straight line ([[10.75]]), then $(x - y)^2$ is a third apotome ([[10.def3.3]]).

**Proof.** $CF = CM - MF$ with @CM $= x^2 + y^2$ and @MF $= 2xy$, as in [[10.97]]. Here both are medial areas, so both breadths are rational and incommensurable with $\rho$ ([[10.22]]). They are incommensurable with each other, because $x^2 : xy = x : y$ is irrational ([[10.11]], [[10.13]]); so @CF is an apotome. As before $CK$, $KM$ are commensurable (since $x^2$, $y^2$ are) and $CK \cdot KM = NM^2$, so $CM^2 - MF^2$ is the square of a line commensurable with $CM$ ([[10.17]]). Neither term is commensurable with $\rho$: $CF$ is third. It mirrors [[10.62]].

---
title: A rational multiple of a bimedial is a bimedial of the same order
---
**Statement.** If @AB is a first (or second) bimedial and @CD is commensurable in length with it, then $CD$ is a first (or second) bimedial.

**Proof.** Cut $CD$ at @F in the ratio of @AE to @EB. Then $CF$, $FD$ are commensurable with $AE$, $EB$, so they are medial ([[10.23]]) and commensurable in square only. Moreover
$$AE^2 : CF^2 = AE\cdot EB : CF\cdot FD,$$
so the rectangle $CF\cdot FD$ is commensurable with $AE \cdot EB$: rational if it was rational (first bimedial), medial if it was medial (second bimedial). In algebra, scaling by $\lambda$ multiplies both the squares and the rectangle by $\lambda^2$, a rational number, and no property in the definitions [[10.37]] and [[10.38]] can notice that.

---
title: A first apotome of a medial has only one annex
---
**Statement.** To a first apotome of a medial ([[10.74]]) only one medial line can be annexed that is commensurable in square only with the whole and contains with it a rational rectangle.

**Proof.** Let @AB have annex @BC and, if possible, also @BD. As in [[10.79]],
$$2\,AD\cdot DB - 2\,AC\cdot CB = (AD^2 + DB^2) - (AC^2 + CB^2).$$
The rectangles are rational, so the left side is rational; the sums of squares are medial ([[10.15]], [[10.23]]). A medial area does not exceed a medial area by a rational one ([[10.26]]). This is [[10.43]] with subtraction.

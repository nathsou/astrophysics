---
title: Medial lines containing a rational rectangle
---
**Statement.** To find medial lines, commensurable in square only, whose rectangle is rational.

With $\rho = 1$, take rationals $A = \sqrt a$, $B = \sqrt b$ commensurable in square only. Then
$$C = \sqrt[4]{ab}, \qquad D = \frac{CB}{A} = \sqrt[4]{b^3/a}$$
are medial, $C : D = A : B$, and $CD = B^2 = b$ is rational.

**Construction and proof.** Let @C be the mean proportional between @A and @B ([[6.13]]): $C^2 = AB$ is medial ([[10.21]]), so $C$ is medial. Take @D with $A : B = C : D$ ([[6.12]]). Then $C, D$ are commensurable in square only ([[10.11]]) and $D$ is medial ([[10.23]]). Alternating, $A : C = B : D$; with $A : C = C : B$ this gives $C : B = B : D$, so $CD = B^2$, rational ([[6.17]]).

These pairs are the terms of the *first bimedial* line ([[10.37]]).

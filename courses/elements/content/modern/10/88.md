---
title: Constructing a fourth apotome
---
**Statement.** With $\rho = 1$, $r$ rational, and numbers $DF$, $FE$ such that $DE = DF + FE$ has to neither of them the ratio of square numbers,
$$r\left(1 - \sqrt{FE/DE}\right)$$
is a fourth apotome ([[10.def3.4]]). With $r = \tfrac32$, $DF = 2$, $FE = 1$ it is $\tfrac32 - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.

**Construction.** Take @BG commensurable with @A and make $DE : EF = BG^2 : GC^2$. As before, @BC is an apotome whose whole is commensurable with $A$. But now, if $BG^2 = GC^2 + H^2$, then $BG^2 : H^2 = ED : DF$, which is not a ratio of squares: @H is *incommensurable* with $BG$ ([[10.9]]). So $BC$ is fourth, not first. It mirrors [[10.51]].

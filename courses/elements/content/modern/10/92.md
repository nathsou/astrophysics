---
title: The side of ρ times a second apotome
---
**Statement.** With $\rho = 1$: if $x - y$ is a second apotome ([[10.def3.2]]), then $\sqrt{x - y}$ is a first apotome of a medial straight line ([[10.74]]). Example: $\sqrt{3\sqrt2 - 4} = \sqrt[4]{8} - \sqrt[4]{2}$.

**Proof.** The construction is that of [[10.91]], giving @LN $= LP - PN$ with $LP^2 = AF$, $PN^2 = FG$ and $LP \cdot PN = EG$. What changes is which lines are rational.

- The whole @AG is incommensurable with $\rho$, and $AF$, $FG$ are commensurable with $AG$ ([[10.17]]). So the rectangles @figure(AI) and @figure(FK) are medial ([[10.21]]), and so are the squares on $LP$, $PN$: these are medial lines, commensurable in square.
- The annex @DG is commensurable with $\rho$, so $EK = LP \cdot PN$ is rational ([[10.19]]).
- A rational area is incommensurable with a medial one, so $LP : PN$, which equals $LO : NO$, is irrational ([[10.11]]).

Medial lines commensurable in square only, containing a rational rectangle: $LN$ is a first apotome of a medial straight line. It mirrors [[10.55]].

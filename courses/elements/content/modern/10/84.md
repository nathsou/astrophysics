---
title: The line producing with a medial area a medial whole has only one annex
---
**Statement.** To the line that produces with a medial area a medial whole ([[10.78]]) only one line can be annexed that is incommensurable in square with the whole, with the sum of the squares medial, twice the rectangle medial, and the two incommensurable.

**Proof.** As in [[10.81]]. Apply @figure(EG) $= AC^2 + CB^2$ to a rational line @EF with breadth @EM, and @figure(HG) $= 2\,AC\cdot CB$ with breadth @HM, so that @figure(EL) $= AB^2$. $EM$ and $MH$ are rational ([[10.22]]) and incommensurable because the two areas are, so @EH is an apotome with annex $HM$. A second annex @BD would give a second annex @HN to the same apotome, contradicting [[10.79]].

[[10.79]]–[[10.84]] make the six subtractive irrationals well defined in the same way that [[10.42]]–[[10.47]] do for the additive ones: each line determines its whole and its annex.

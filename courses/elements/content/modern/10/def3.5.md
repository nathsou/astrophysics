---
title: Fifth apotome
---
An apotome $x - y$ is **fifth** when $\sqrt{x^2 - y^2}$ is incommensurable with the whole $x$, and the annex $y$ is commensurable with the rational line $\rho$.

Example: $\sqrt2 - 1$. Here $\sqrt{2 - 1} = 1$ is incommensurable with $\sqrt2$, and the annex $1$ is rational.

The square root of $\rho$ times a fifth apotome is "that which produces with a rational area a medial whole" ([[10.95]]). See the table under [[10.def3.1]].

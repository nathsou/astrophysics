---
title: The side of a rational plus a medial area splits in only one way
---
**Statement.** The side of a rational plus a medial area ([[10.40]]) is $x + y$ with $x, y$ incommensurable in square, $x^2 + y^2$ medial and $2xy$ rational. It can be so cut at one point only.

**Proof.** Suppose @AB is cut so at @C and at @D. By [[2.4]],
$$2\,AC\cdot CB - 2\,AD\cdot DB = (AD^2 + DB^2) - (AC^2 + CB^2).$$
The rectangles are rational, so the left side is rational; the sums of squares are medial. This contradicts [[10.26]]. The argument is that of [[10.43]], with the sum of squares medial and the rectangle rational.

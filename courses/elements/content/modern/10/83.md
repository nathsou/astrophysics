---
title: The line producing with a rational area a medial whole has only one annex
---
**Statement.** To the line that produces with a rational area a medial whole ([[10.77]]) only one line can be annexed that is incommensurable in square with the whole and makes the sum of the squares medial and twice the rectangle rational.

**Proof.** Let @AB have annex @BC and, if possible, also @BD. As in [[10.79]], the rectangles $2\,AD\cdot DB$ and $2\,AC\cdot CB$ differ by the same area as the sums of squares. The rectangles are rational, so that difference is rational, while the sums of squares are medial. This contradicts [[10.26]], as in [[10.46]].

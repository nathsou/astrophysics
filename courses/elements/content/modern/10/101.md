---
title: The square of the fifth subtractive line, applied to ρ
---
**Statement.** With $\rho = 1$: if $x - y$ "produces with a rational area a medial whole" ([[10.77]]), then $(x - y)^2$ is a fifth apotome ([[10.def3.5]]). Example: $\sqrt{\sqrt2 - 1}$ squared is $\sqrt2 - 1$.

**Proof.** $CF = CM - MF$ with @CM $= x^2 + y^2$ medial, so rational but incommensurable with $\rho$ ([[10.22]]), and @MF $= 2xy$ rational, so commensurable with $\rho$ ([[10.20]]). They are incommensurable, so @CF is an apotome whose annex is commensurable with $\rho$. The parts $CK$, $KM$ are incommensurable (as $x^2$, $y^2$ are), so by [[10.18]] $CF$ is fifth. It mirrors [[10.64]].

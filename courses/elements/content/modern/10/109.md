---
title: Medial minus rational
---
**Statement.** With $\rho = 1$: if a rational area is taken from a medial area, the side of the remainder is a first apotome of a medial straight line or "that which produces with a rational area a medial whole".

**Proof.** As in [[10.108]], apply the areas to the rational line @FG. Now @FH is rational but incommensurable with $\rho$ ([[10.22]]), and @FK is commensurable with $\rho$ ([[10.20]]). So @KH is an apotome whose *annex* is commensurable with $\rho$: second or fifth. By [[10.92]] and [[10.95]], the side of @figure(EC) is a first apotome of a medial or the fifth subtractive line.

Example: $\sqrt3 - \tfrac32$ is second, since $3 - \tfrac94 = \tfrac34$ and $\sqrt{3/4} = \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$ is commensurable with $\sqrt3$; so $\sqrt{\sqrt3 - 3/2}$ is a first apotome of a medial. But $\sqrt3 - 1$ is fifth, because $\sqrt2$ is incommensurable with $\sqrt3$.

---
title: Constructing a sixth apotome
---
**Statement.** With $\rho = 1$, take numbers $E$, $BC$, $CD$, no two of them in the ratio of square numbers, and with $CB : BD$ not a ratio of squares either, where $BD = BC - CD$. Then
$$\sqrt{BC/E} - \sqrt{CD/E}$$
is a sixth apotome ([[10.def3.6]]). With $E = 2$, $BC = 6$, $CD = 1$ it is $\sqrt3 - \sqrt{\tfrac12}$.

**Construction.** Exactly as in [[10.87]]: $E : BC = A^2 : FG^2$ and $BC : CD = FG^2 : GH^2$ make @FH an apotome neither of whose terms is commensurable with @A. The one change is in the last step: $FG^2 : K^2 = CB : BD$ is not a ratio of squares, so @K is incommensurable with $FG$, and $FH$ is sixth. It mirrors [[10.53]].

The six constructions together show that none of the six kinds of apotome is empty, which the classification of [[10.91]]–[[10.96]] needs.

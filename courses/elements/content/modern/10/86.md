---
title: Constructing a second apotome
---
**Statement.** With $\rho = 1$, $r$ rational and $m > n$ integers with $m^2 - n^2$ not a square,
$$r\left(\frac{m}{\sqrt{m^2 - n^2}} - 1\right)$$
is a second apotome ([[10.def3.2]]). This time the *annex* is the rational one.

**Construction.** Take the annex @CG commensurable with @A, square numbers @DE, @EF with @DF not a square, and make $FD : DE = CG^2 : GB^2$. As in [[10.85]], @BG is rational and incommensurable in length with $CG$, so @BC is an apotome. If $BG^2 = GC^2 + H^2$, then $BG^2 : H^2 = DE : EF$ is a ratio of squares, so @H is commensurable with $BG$. The annex $CG$ is commensurable with $A$, so $BC$ is second. It mirrors [[10.49]].

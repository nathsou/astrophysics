---
title: Constructing a fifth apotome
---
**Statement.** With $\rho = 1$, $r$ rational, and numbers $DF$, $FE$ such that $DE = DF + FE$ has to neither of them the ratio of square numbers,
$$r\left(\sqrt{DE/FE} - 1\right)$$
is a fifth apotome ([[10.def3.5]]). With $r = \tfrac54$, $DF = 4$, $FE = 1$ it is $\tfrac54(\sqrt5 - 1)$.

**Construction.** Take the annex @CG commensurable with @A and make $FE : ED = CG^2 : GB^2$. Then @BC is an apotome whose annex is commensurable with $A$, and $BG^2 : H^2 = ED : DF$ is not a ratio of squares, so @H is incommensurable with the whole $BG$. So $BC$ is fifth. It mirrors [[10.52]].

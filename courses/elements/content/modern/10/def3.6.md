---
title: Sixth apotome
---
An apotome $x - y$ is **sixth** when $\sqrt{x^2 - y^2}$ is incommensurable with the whole $x$, and neither $x$ nor $y$ is commensurable with the rational line $\rho$.

Example: $\sqrt3 - \sqrt2$. Here $\sqrt{3 - 2} = 1$ is incommensurable with $\sqrt3$, and neither term is rational.

The square root of $\rho$ times a sixth apotome is "that which produces with a medial area a medial whole" ([[10.96]]). See the table under [[10.def3.1]].

---
title: A line commensurable with the side of the sum of two medial areas is another
---
**Statement.** If @AB is the side of the sum of two medial areas and @CD is commensurable with it, then $CD$ is also the side of the sum of two medial areas.

**Proof.** As in [[10.68]]: cut $CD$ at @F in the ratio in which @E cuts $AB$. Both the sum of squares and the rectangle are multiplied by the same rational number $\lambda^2$, so both remain medial and they remain incommensurable with each other ([[10.41]]).

With [[10.66]]–[[10.69]], this shows that each of the six additive irrationals is a type closed under multiplication by rationals, just as the rational and medial lines are ([[10.23]]).

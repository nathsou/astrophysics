---
title: Medial lines commensurable in length give a medial rectangle
---
**Statement.** If $AB, BC$ are medial and commensurable in length, the rectangle $AB \cdot BC$ is medial. With $\rho = 1$: $AB = a\sqrt[4]k$, $BC = b\sqrt[4]k$ give $AB \cdot BC = ab\sqrt k$.

**Proof.** As in [[10.19]]: the square @figure(AD) on @AB is medial, $AD : AC = BD : BC$ is a ratio of numbers, so the rectangle @figure(AC) is commensurable with a medial area and is medial by the porism of [[10.23]].

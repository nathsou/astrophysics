---
title: The square of the side of a rational plus a medial area gives a fifth binomial
---
**Statement.** If @AB is the side of a rational plus a medial area, cut at @C, then $AB^2$ applied to a rational line @DE $= 1$ has as breadth @DG a fifth binomial. Converse of [[10.58]].

**Proof.** Same construction as [[10.60]]. $AC^2 + CB^2$ is medial, so @DM is rational and incommensurable with $DE$ ([[10.22]]); $2\,AC\cdot CB$ is rational, so @MG is commensurable with $DE$ ([[10.20]]). $AC^2$ and $CB^2$ are incommensurable, so @DK and @KM are, and $\sqrt{DM^2 - MG^2}$ is incommensurable with $DM$ ([[10.18]]). The lesser term is commensurable with $DE$: a fifth binomial.

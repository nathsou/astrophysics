---
title: Third binomial
---
A binomial $x + y$ is **third** when $\sqrt{x^2 - y^2}$ is commensurable with $x$, but neither term is commensurable with the rational line $\rho$.

Example: $3\sqrt2 + \sqrt{10}$. Here $x^2 - y^2 = 18 - 10 = 8$ and $\sqrt8 / 3\sqrt2 = 2/3$ is rational, while $3\sqrt2$ and $\sqrt{10}$ are both irrational numbers.

The square root of $\rho$ times a third binomial is a second bimedial ([[10.56]]). See the table under [[10.def2.1]].

---
title: Fourth apotome
---
An apotome $x - y$ is **fourth** when $\sqrt{x^2 - y^2}$ is *incommensurable* with the whole $x$, and $x$ is commensurable with the rational line $\rho$.

Example: $2 - \sqrt2$. Here $\sqrt{4 - 2} = \sqrt2$, and $\sqrt2/2$ is irrational.

The fourth, fifth and sixth apotomes are those for which the rectangle of [[10.18]] cuts the whole into incommensurable parts. The square root of $\rho$ times a fourth apotome is a minor straight line ([[10.94]]). See the table under [[10.def3.1]].

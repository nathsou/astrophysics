---
title: The side of ρ times a fifth apotome
---
**Statement.** With $\rho = 1$: if $x - y$ is a fifth apotome ([[10.def3.5]]), then $\sqrt{x - y}$ is "that which produces with a rational area a medial whole" ([[10.77]]). Example: $\sqrt{\sqrt2 - 1}$.

**Proof.** Same construction as [[10.91]]. As in [[10.94]], $AF$ and $FG$ are incommensurable ([[10.18]]), so $LP$ and $PN$ are incommensurable in square. The difference is where the rational line sits:

- the whole @AG is incommensurable with $\rho$, so $LP^2 + PN^2 = $ @figure(AK) is medial ([[10.21]]);
- the annex @DG is commensurable with $\rho$, so $2\,LP \cdot PN = $ @figure(DK) is rational ([[10.19]]).

The name describes the line $z = LP - PN$ by what it does: $z^2 = (LP^2 + PN^2) - 2\,LP \cdot PN$, so the square on @LN together with the rational area $2\,LP \cdot PN$ makes up the medial whole $LP^2 + PN^2$. It mirrors [[10.58]].

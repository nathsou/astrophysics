---
title: Third apotome
---
An apotome $x - y$ is **third** when $\sqrt{x^2 - y^2}$ is commensurable with the whole $x$, and *neither* $x$ nor $y$ is commensurable with the rational line $\rho$.

Example: $3\sqrt2 - \sqrt{10}$. Here $\sqrt{18 - 10} = 2\sqrt2$, commensurable with $3\sqrt2$, and neither term is a rational number.

The square root of $\rho$ times a third apotome is a second apotome of a medial straight line ([[10.93]]). See the table under [[10.def3.1]].

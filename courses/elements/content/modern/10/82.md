---
title: A minor has only one annex
---
**Statement.** To a minor ([[10.76]]) only one line can be annexed that is incommensurable in square with the whole and makes the sum of the squares rational and twice the rectangle medial.

**Proof.** Let @AB have annex @BC and, if possible, also @BD. As in [[10.79]], $(AD^2 + DB^2) - (AC^2 + CB^2) = 2\,AD\cdot DB - 2\,AC\cdot CB$. The sums of squares are rational, the rectangles medial, and [[10.26]] rules this out. The argument is that of [[10.45]] for the major.

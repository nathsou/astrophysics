---
title: That which produces with a medial area a medial whole
---
**Statement.** Let @AB and @BC be incommensurable in square, with $AB^2 + BC^2$ medial, $2\,AB\cdot BC$ medial, and the two incommensurable ([[10.35]]). The remainder @AC $= AB - BC$ is irrational: its square plus the medial area $2\,AB\cdot BC$ makes a medial whole. It is the side of the sum of two medial areas ([[10.41]]) with a minus sign; the default is $\sqrt{\sqrt5 - \sqrt2}$.

**Proof.** As in [[10.75]], apply the areas to a rational line @DI: @figure(DE) $= AB^2 + BC^2$ with breadth @DG, @figure(DH) $= 2\,AB\cdot BC$ with breadth @DF, and the rest @figure(FE) $= AC^2$. Both breadths are rational, incommensurable in length with $DI$ ([[10.22]]), and incommensurable with each other because the areas are. So @FG is an apotome ([[10.73]]), the rectangle $FE$ on a rational line and an apotome is irrational, and $AC$ is irrational.

This completes the six subtractive irrationals. With the six additive ones of [[10.36]]–[[10.41]] and the medial line, they are the thirteen irrationals of Book X.

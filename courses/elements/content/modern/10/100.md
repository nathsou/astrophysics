---
title: The square of a minor line, applied to ρ
---
**Statement.** With $\rho = 1$: if $x - y$ is minor ([[10.76]]), then $(x - y)^2$ is a fourth apotome ([[10.def3.4]]). Example: $\left(\sqrt{1 + \tfrac{\sqrt2}{2}} - \sqrt{1 - \tfrac{\sqrt2}{2}}\right)^2 = 2 - \sqrt2$.

**Proof.** $CF = CM - MF$ with @CM $= x^2 + y^2$ rational, so commensurable with $\rho$ ([[10.20]]), and @MF $= 2xy$ medial, so rational but incommensurable with $\rho$ ([[10.22]]). So @CF is an apotome whose whole is commensurable with $\rho$. This time $x^2$ and $y^2$ are incommensurable, so $CK$, $KM$ are incommensurable, and [[10.18]] makes $\sqrt{CM^2 - MF^2}$ incommensurable with $CM$. So $CF$ is fourth. It mirrors [[10.63]].

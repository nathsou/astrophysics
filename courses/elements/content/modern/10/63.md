---
title: The square of a major gives a fourth binomial
---
**Statement.** If @AB is a major cut at @C, then $AB^2$ applied to a rational line @DE $= 1$ has as breadth @DG a fourth binomial. Converse of [[10.57]].

**Proof.** Same construction as [[10.60]]. $AC^2 + CB^2$ is rational, so @DM is commensurable with $DE$; $2\,AC\cdot CB$ is medial, so @MG is rational and incommensurable with $DE$. So $DG$ is a binomial with $DM$ the greater term. What changes is @DK and @KM: $AC^2$ and $CB^2$ are now incommensurable, so $DK$, $KM$ are too, and by [[10.18]] $\sqrt{DM^2 - MG^2}$ is incommensurable with $DM$. The greater term is commensurable with $DE$: a fourth binomial.

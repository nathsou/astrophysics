---
title: Constructing a third apotome
---
**Statement.** With $\rho = 1$, take numbers $E$, $BC$, $CD$, no two of them in the ratio of square numbers, but with $BC : BD$ a ratio of squares, where $BD = BC - CD$. Then
$$\sqrt{BC/E} - \sqrt{CD/E}$$
is a third apotome ([[10.def3.3]]). With $E = 2$, $BC = 9$, $CD = 5$ it is $\tfrac{3}{\sqrt2} - \sqrt{\tfrac52}$.

**Construction.** Make $E : BC = A^2 : FG^2$ and $BC : CD = FG^2 : GH^2$ ([[10.6]]). Then @FG and @GH are rational, incommensurable in length with each other, and neither is commensurable with the rational line @A ([[10.9]]; for $GH$ use $E : CD = A^2 : GH^2$, ex aequali). So @FH is an apotome, and neither of its terms is commensurable with $A$. If $FG^2 = GH^2 + K^2$, then $FG^2 : K^2 = BC : BD$ is a ratio of squares, so @K is commensurable with $FG$. So $FH$ is third. It mirrors [[10.50]].

---
title: Cubes fail to divide iff their sides fail
---
**Statement.** For numbers $c, d$:
$$c^3 \nmid d^3 \iff c \nmid d.$$

**Proof.** This is [[8.15]] negated on both sides. If @C divided @D, @A would divide @B, and conversely.

With the defaults, $64 \nmid 216$ and $4 \nmid 6$. The two cubes share the factor $8$, but $216 = 3 \cdot 64 + 24$.

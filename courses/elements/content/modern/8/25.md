---
title: In the ratio of two cubes
---
**Statement.** If $a : b = c^3 : d^3$ and $a$ is a cube, then $b$ is a cube.

**Proof.** $C$ and $D$ are cubes, hence similar solid numbers, so two means fall between them ([[8.19]]). By [[8.8]], two means @E, @F fall between @A and @B as well. $A, E, F, B$ is a progression starting with a cube, so $B$ is a cube ([[8.23]]).

With the defaults, $C : D = 64 : 216 = 8 : 27$. The means between $C$ and $D$ (thin rods) are $96$ and $144$, and those between $A = 8$ and $B = 27$ are $12$ and $18$.

---
title: The side of the inscribed triangle
---
**Statement.** The square on the side of an equilateral triangle inscribed in a circle is three times the square on the radius: $AB^2 = 3r^2$, so $AB = \sqrt3\,r$.

**Proof.** Let @D be the centre and extend @AD to @E. The arc $BEC$ is a third of the circle, so the arc $BE$ is a sixth, and @BE is the side of the hexagon, equal to the radius @DE ([[4.15]]). The angle $ABE$ is in a semicircle, so it is right ([[3.31]]), and $AE^2 = AB^2 + BE^2$ ([[1.47]]). Since $AE = 2DE$, $AE^2 = 4BE^2$, so $AB^2 = 3BE^2 = 3DE^2$.

:::leads Where it leads

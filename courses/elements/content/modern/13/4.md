---
title: Golden section, third lemma
---
**Statement.** If @AB is cut in extreme and mean ratio at @C (@AC greater), then
$$AB^2 + BC^2 = 3\,AC^2.$$

**Proof.** In the square $ADEB$, the diagonal @DB and the lines through $C$ and @F give the square @figure(HG) on $AC$ and the square @figure(CK) on $BC$. The rectangle @figure(AK) is $AB \cdot BC = AC^2$, and its equal complement makes @figure(CE) equal to it too. The gnomon @LMN with the square $CK$ is $AK + CE = 2AC^2$, and adding $HG = AC^2$ gives the two squares $AB^2 + BC^2$ on one side and $3AC^2$ on the other.

With $AB = 1$ and $x = AC$, the golden cut says $BC = 1 - x = x^2$. So $AB^2 + BC^2 = 1 + (1 - x)^2 = 2(1 - x) + x^2 = 3x^2$.

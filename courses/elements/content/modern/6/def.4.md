---
title: Height
---
The **height** of a figure is the perpendicular distance from its vertex (the point farthest from the base) to the line of the base.

This is what "under the same height" means in [[6.1]]. Two triangles whose apexes lie on one line parallel to their common base line have the same height. The definition fits triangles and parallelograms. For other figures "the vertex" is not well defined, and Euclid only ever uses heights of triangles and parallelograms.

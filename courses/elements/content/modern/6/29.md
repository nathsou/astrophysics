---
title: Application with excess (solving x(a + x) = b²)
---
**Problem.** Given a segment $AB$, a figure @C and a parallelogram @D, apply to $AB$ a parallelogram equal to $C$ and *exceeding* by a parallelogram similar to $D$. The applied parallelogram stands on $AB$ extended beyond $B$, and the excess is the part beyond $B$.

In the simplest case (rectangles, square excess), with $AB = a$, $C = b^2$ and excess side $x$:
$$x(a + x) = b^2, \qquad x = \sqrt{\left(\tfrac a2\right)^2 + b^2} - \tfrac a2.$$
Unlike [[6.28]], there is no condition: the equation $x^2 + ax - b^2 = 0$ always has exactly one positive root, since its discriminant $a^2 + 4b^2$ is always positive.

**Construction (Euclid's).**

1. Bisect $AB$ at @E and describe @figure(BF) $= EBLF$ on $EB$ similar to $D$.
2. Construct @figure(GH) similar to $D$ and equal to $BF + C$ ([[6.25]]).
3. Produce @FL and @FE so that $FM = KH$ and $FN = KG$, and complete @figure(MN). It is a copy of $GH$ in the corner $F$, so it is about the same diameter @FO as $BF$ ([[6.26]]), and @B lies on $FO$.
4. The answer is @figure(AO), exceeding by @figure(QP), which is similar to $D$ ([[6.24]]).

**Why it works.** $MN = GH = BF + C$, so the gnomon @figure(XWV) $= MN - BF = C$. Since $AE = EB$, @figure(AN) $=$ @figure(NB) ([[1.36]]), and $NB =$ @figure(LP) as complements ([[1.43]]). Adding @figure(EO) to both: $AO = EO + AN = EO + LP$, which is the gnomon. So $AO = C$.

:::modern Completing the square, again
In the square case $BF = (a/2)^2$ and $MN = (a/2)^2 + b^2$, so $FM = \sqrt{(a/2)^2 + b^2}$, and the excess has side $BP = FM - FL = \sqrt{(a/2)^2 + b^2} - \tfrac a2$. The identity behind the gnomon is
$$x(a + x) = \left(\tfrac a2 + x\right)^2 - \left(\tfrac a2\right)^2,$$
which is [[2.6]] for rectangles. [[6.30]] applies this construction with $C$ equal to the square on $AB$ to find the golden section.
:::

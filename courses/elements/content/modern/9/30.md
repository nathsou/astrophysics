---
title: An odd divisor of an even number divides its half
---
**Statement.** If $a$ is odd, $b$ is even and $a \mid b$, then $a \mid b/2$.

**Proof.** Let $B = A \cdot C$. If @C were odd, @B would be an odd count of odd numbers, and so odd ([[9.23]]). So $C$ is even, $C = 2c$, and $B/2 = A \cdot c$.

In modern terms: $a \mid 2m$ with $\gcd(a, 2) = 1$ gives $a \mid m$. This is Euclid's lemma ([[7.30]]) for the prime $2$, proved here by parity alone.

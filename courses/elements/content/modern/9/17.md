---
title: A continued proportion with coprime extremes cannot be extended
---
**Statement.** If $A, B, C, D$ are in continued proportion and the extremes $A, D$ are coprime, there is no number $E$ with $A : B = D : E$. The sequence cannot be continued.

Such a sequence is $x^3, x^2 y, x y^2, y^3$ with $\gcd(x, y) = 1$, and $E$ would have to be $y^4/x$.

**Proof.** Suppose @E existed. Alternately, $A : D = B : E$ ([[7.13]]). $A$ and $D$ are coprime, hence least in their ratio ([[7.21]]), so $A \mid B$ ([[7.20]]). Along the sequence, $A \mid B$ gives $B \mid C$ and $C \mid D$, since consecutive ratios are equal. So $A \mid D$, while $A$ and $D$ are coprime. Contradiction.

[[9.16]] is the case of two terms.

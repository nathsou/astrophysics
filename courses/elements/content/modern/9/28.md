---
title: Odd times even is even
---
**Statement.** If $a$ is odd and $b$ is even, $ab$ is even.

**Proof.** @C $= A \cdot B$ is @B added to itself $A$ times ([[7.def.15]]), a sum of even numbers, so it is even ([[9.21]]). The copies of $B$ are marked on $C$.

The oddness of @A is not used: any multiple of an even number is even.

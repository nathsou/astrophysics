---
title: If the first power is a square, all are
---
**Statement.** If $1, A, B, C, \dots$ are in continued proportion and $A$ is a square, every term is a square. If $A$ is a cube, every term is a cube.

**Proof.** The terms are $A^k$. For squares: $A, B, C$ are in continued proportion and $A$ is a square, so @C is a square ([[8.22]]); then $B, C, D$ give @D, and so on. The even-numbered terms are squares already by [[9.8]].

For cubes: $B = A^2$ is a cube by [[9.3]]. Then $A, B, C, D$ are in continued proportion with $A$ a cube, so @D is a cube ([[8.23]]). The same argument works for every later term.

In exponents: if $A = s^2$, then $A^k = (s^k)^2$. Move the first slider to 1 to see the cube case.

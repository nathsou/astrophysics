---
title: If a square is a cube, its root is a cube
---
**Statement.** If $A^2$ is a cube, then $A$ is a cube.

**Proof.** Let @B $= A^2$ and @C $= AB = A^3$, a cube by definition ([[7.def.19]]). Now
$$1 : A = A : B = B : C,$$
because each term is $A$ times the one before ([[7.def.20]]). $B$ and $C$ are cubes, so two means fall between them ([[8.19]]). $A : B = B : C$, so two means fall between $A$ and $B$ as well ([[8.8]]). Since $B$ is a cube, [[8.23]] (read from the other end) makes $A$ a cube.

**In modern terms.** If $2e$ is a multiple of $3$, then $e$ is a multiple of $3$, where $e$ is the exponent of any prime in $A$. Euclid's proof avoids factorization entirely.

---
title: Coprime numbers have no third proportional
---
**Statement.** If $A$ and $B$ are coprime, there is no number $C$ with $A : B = B : C$. Equivalently, $A \nmid B^2$ (for $A \ge 2$).

**Proof.** Suppose @C existed. @A, @B are coprime, hence the least numbers in their ratio ([[7.21]]). The least numbers divide every pair in the same ratio, term by term ([[7.20]]), so $A \mid B$. Then $A$ divides both $A$ and $B$, which are coprime. Contradiction.

In the figure the dashed @C is $B^2/A$, which is never a whole number.

---
title: Coprime to a number, coprime to its double
---
**Statement.** If $a$ is odd and $\gcd(a, b) = 1$, then $\gcd(a, 2b) = 1$.

**Proof.** Suppose some number @D divides both @A and @C $= 2B$. $D$ divides the odd number $A$, so $D$ is odd (a multiple of an even number is a sum of even numbers, hence even, [[9.21]]). An odd number that divides the even number $C$ divides its half @B ([[9.30]]). So $D$ divides $A$ and $B$, which are coprime. Contradiction.

The dashed $D$ is the supposed common divisor.

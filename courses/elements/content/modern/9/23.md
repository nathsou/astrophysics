---
title: An odd count of odd numbers sums to odd
---
**Statement.** The sum of an odd number of odd numbers is odd.

**Proof.** Take the unit @DE from the last number @CD. Then @CE is even, and @CA, the sum of the others (an even count of odd numbers), is even ([[9.22]]). So @AE is even ([[9.21]]), and @AD $= AE + 1$ is odd.

---
title: Twice an odd number is even-times odd only
---
**Statement.** If the half of @A is odd, then $A$ is even-times odd ([[7.def.9]]) and not even-times even ([[7.def.8]]).

**Proof.** The odd half divides $A$ twice, an even number of times, so $A$ is even-times odd. If $A = d \cdot q$ with both $d$ and $q$ even, then $A/2 = d \cdot (q/2)$ would be divisible by the even number $d$, although it is odd.

In modern terms, these are the numbers $n = 2m$ with $m$ odd, the numbers with exactly one factor $2$.

---
title: An even count of odd numbers sums to even
---
**Statement.** The sum of an even number of odd numbers is even.

**Proof.** Take a unit from each of @AB, @BC, @CD, @DE. Each remainder is even ([[7.def.7]]), so the remainders add to an even number ([[9.21]]). The units removed are even in multitude, so they also make an even number. The whole @AE is the sum of the two, so it is even ([[9.21]]).

In modern notation, $\sum_{i=1}^{2m} (2a_i + 1) = 2\left(\sum a_i + m\right)$.

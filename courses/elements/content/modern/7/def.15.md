---
title: Multiplication
---
"$a$ multiplies $b$" means: add $b$ to itself as many times as there are units in $a$. So $a \times b = \underbrace{b + b + \dots + b}_{a \text{ times}}$.

Multiplication is defined by repeated addition, and the definition is not symmetric: $a \times b$ is $a$ copies of $b$, $b \times a$ is $b$ copies of $a$. That they are equal is a theorem, [[7.16]]. The same situation arises in Peano arithmetic, where $a \cdot (b+1) = a\cdot b + a$ is the definition, and commutativity has to be proved by induction.

---
title: Every number is prime or has a prime divisor
---
**Statement.** Every number $a$ is either prime or divisible by some prime.

**Proof.** If @A is prime, we are done. If it is composite, [[7.31]] gives a prime divisor.

Together with the unit being excluded from numbers, this says: every $n \ge 2$ has a prime divisor (itself, if it is prime). Applied repeatedly to the quotients, it shows that every number is a product of primes, the existence half of unique factorization ([[7.30]]). [[9.20]] uses it to show that there are infinitely many primes: a prime divisor of $p_1 p_2 \cdots p_k + 1$ is none of the $p_i$.

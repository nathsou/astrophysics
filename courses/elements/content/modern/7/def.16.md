---
title: Plane number
---
The product of two numbers is a **plane** number, and the two numbers are its **sides**: $6 = 2 \cdot 3$ is a plane number with sides $2$ and $3$. The name comes from picturing the product as a rectangle of $2 \times 3$ dots. A plane number usually has several pairs of sides ($12 = 2 \cdot 6 = 3 \cdot 4$), and with the unit excluded, the plane numbers are exactly the composite numbers.

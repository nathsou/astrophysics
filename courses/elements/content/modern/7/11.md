---
title: Proportional remainders
---
**Statement.** If $a : b = a' : b'$ with $a' < a$ and $b' < b$, then $(a - a') : (b - b') = a : b$. In the figure, $AB : CD = AE : CF$ implies $EB : FD = AB : CD$.

**Proof.** By [[7.def.20]], @AE is the same part or parts of @CF that @AB is of @CD. So by [[7.7]] (part) or [[7.8]] (parts), the remainder @EB is that same part or parts of @FD. By [[7.def.20]] again, $EB : FD = AB : CD$.

With fractions: if $a/b = a'/b' = r$, then $a - a' = r b - r b' = r(b - b')$. The magnitude version is [[5.19]].

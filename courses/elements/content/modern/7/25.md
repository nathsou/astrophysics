---
title: The square of a number prime to b is prime to b
---
**Statement.** If $\gcd(a, b) = 1$, then $\gcd(a^2, b) = 1$.

**Proof.** Let @D be a copy of @A. Both $D$ and $A$ are prime to @B, so their product @C $= A \cdot A$ is prime to $B$ by [[7.24]].

It is [[7.24]] with both factors equal. Euclid introduces the copy @D so that "two numbers" can be the same number.

---
title: Part (divisor)
---
A number $a$ is a **part** of a larger number $b$ when $a$ measures $b$, that is, $a \mid b$ and $a < b$. Then $b = n a$, and $a$ is "the $n$-th part" of $b$: a half, a third, a quarter…

A part is a proper divisor, or equivalently a unit fraction of $b$: $a = \tfrac1n b$. Euclid also counts the unit as a part (in [[7.4]], each unit of a number is "some part" of a larger one). "Measures" is the verb that runs through Books VII–IX, and it is translated throughout as "divides", $a \mid b$.

---
title: Unit
---
The unit is $1$: what makes a thing count as one thing. For Euclid it is **not a number**. Numbers start at $2$ ([[7.def.2]]), and zero does not exist at all.

Separating the unit from the numbers has consequences throughout Books VII–IX. A statement about "numbers" silently excludes $1$, so the unit often needs its own proposition or its own case: [[7.1]] (the algorithm ends at the unit) is separate from [[7.2]] (it ends at a number), and [[7.15]] is a lemma specifically about the unit. The unit can still *measure* numbers, and it counts as a "part" ([[7.4]]).

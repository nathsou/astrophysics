---
title: Even-times odd number
---
A number is **even-times odd** when it is the product of an even number and an odd number, $n = a b$ with $a$ even and $b$ odd, for example $6 = 2 \cdot 3$ or $20 = 4 \cdot 5$. Book IX singles out the numbers that are even-times odd *only*: those of the form $2 \cdot \text{odd}$ ([[9.33]]).

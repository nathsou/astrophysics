---
title: Sum of proportionals (mediant)
---
**Statement.** If $a : b = c : d$, then $a : b = (a + c) : (b + d)$. The same holds for any number of equal ratios.

**Proof.** @A is some part or parts of @B, and @C is the same part or parts of @D ([[7.def.20]]). By [[7.5]] or [[7.6]], $A + C$ is the same part or parts of $B + D$.

The fraction $\frac{a+c}{b+d}$ is called the *mediant* of $\frac ab$ and $\frac cd$. When the two fractions are equal, the mediant equals them, which is this proposition. When they differ, the mediant lies strictly between them. That fact generates the Stern–Brocot tree and the Farey sequences, which list every positive fraction in lowest terms exactly once. The magnitude version is [[5.12]].

---
title: Similar plane and solid numbers
---
Plane numbers (or solid numbers) are **similar** when their sides are proportional. $6 = 2 \cdot 3$ and $24 = 4 \cdot 6$ are similar, because $2 : 3 = 4 : 6$: their rectangles of dots have the same shape. Similar plane numbers have the same ratio as two squares ([[8.26]]), and between two similar plane numbers there is always a mean proportional ([[8.18]]).

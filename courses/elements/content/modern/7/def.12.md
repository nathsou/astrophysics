---
title: Numbers prime to one another (coprime)
---
Numbers are **prime to one another** when their only common measure is the unit: $\gcd(a, b) = 1$. Neither number has to be prime: $8$ and $15$ are prime to one another.

This is one of the most used notions of Books VII–IX. [[7.1]] tests it with repeated subtraction, [[7.21]] and [[7.22]] show that coprime pairs are exactly the fractions in lowest terms, and [[7.23]]–[[7.28]] show how coprimality behaves under products and sums.

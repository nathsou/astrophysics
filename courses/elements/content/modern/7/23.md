---
title: A divisor of one of two coprime numbers is prime to the other
---
**Statement.** If $\gcd(a, b) = 1$ and $c \mid a$, then $\gcd(c, b) = 1$.

**Proof.** A number @D dividing both @C and @B would divide @A, because $D \mid C$ and $C \mid A$. So $D$ would be a common measure of @A and @B, which are prime to one another.

Divisibility is transitive, and so any common divisor of $c$ and $b$ is a common divisor of $a$ and $b$. This small lemma is used in [[7.24]].

---
title: Numbers composite to one another
---
Numbers are **composite to one another** when some number (not just the unit) measures them all: $\gcd(a, b) > 1$. It is the negation of [[7.def.12]]. For example, $9$ and $15$ are composite to one another, since both are measured by $3$. [[7.2]] finds the greatest such common measure.

---
title: Triangles by their sides
---
A triangle is *equilateral* if all three sides are equal, *isosceles* if exactly two are ("two of its sides alone"), and *scalene* if no two are.

Euclid's classes are exclusive: an equilateral triangle is not isosceles. The modern convention is inclusive, with isosceles meaning "at least two sides equal", so that theorems about isosceles triangles, like [[1.5]], apply to equilateral ones as well.

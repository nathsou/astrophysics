---
title: Ends of a line
---
The endpoints of a line are points.

Euclid's lines are usually finite: what we call a *segment*, or a curve with two ends. The definition connects [[1.def.1]] and [[1.def.2]], one dimension to the next, as [[1.def.6]] will do for surfaces. Not every line has ends, a circle for instance, so it is best read as "if a line has ends, they are points".

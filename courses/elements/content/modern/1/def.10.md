---
title: Right angle, perpendicular
---
If a line standing on another makes the two adjacent angles equal, each is a *right angle*, and the standing line is *perpendicular* to the other.

This is a definition by symmetry, not by measure: a right angle is an angle equal to its own supplement. Two questions are left open. That right angles exist is proved in [[1.11]]. That all right angles are equal, wherever they are, is Postulate 4 ([[1.post.4]]). Only after that can "two right angles" serve as a common yardstick, in [[1.13]], [[1.17]], [[1.32]] and in Postulate 5.

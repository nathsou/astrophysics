---
title: Rectilineal figures
---
Rectilineal figures are figures bounded by straight lines: *trilateral* (three sides, triangles), *quadrilateral* (four), *multilateral* (more than four).

These are the polygons. Euclid silently assumes they are *simple*: the boundary does not cross itself, so that the figure has an inside with an area. A self-crossing "pentagon", the pentagram, is not a rectilineal figure in his sense.

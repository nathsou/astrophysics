---
title: Figure
---
A figure is a region enclosed by one or more boundaries.

For Euclid a figure is the *region*, not its outline: a triangle is a piece of the plane with three sides. That is why "the triangle is equal to the triangle" ([[1.4]]) means equal in *area*, and why Euclid can add and subtract figures with the common notions. A circle ([[1.def.15]]) is likewise the disk, and its outline is called the circumference.

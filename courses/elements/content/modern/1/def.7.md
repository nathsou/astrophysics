---
title: Plane
---
A plane is a surface that "lies evenly with the straight lines on itself".

The wording copies [[1.def.4]] and is just as obscure. A usable version is: a surface such that the straight line joining any two of its points lies wholly in it. Books I–VI take place in a single plane, and Euclid never needs to say so. Book XI, on solid geometry, is where planes have to be handled explicitly.

---
title: Surface
---
A surface is a two-dimensional object: it has length and width, but no depth.

With [[1.def.1]] and [[1.def.2]] this makes a ladder of dimensions: point (0), line (1), surface (2), and, in Book XI, solid (3). Each is described by what it lacks. Modern geometry defines dimension properly (for manifolds, as the number of coordinates needed locally), but Euclid only needs the intuition.

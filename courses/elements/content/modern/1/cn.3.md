---
title: Subtracting equals from equals
---
If $a = b$ and $c = d$, with $c$ a part of $a$ and $d$ a part of $b$, then $a - c = b - d$.

Euclid's magnitudes are never negative, so subtraction is only defined when the part is smaller. [[1.2]] and [[1.15]] use this notion. Adding and subtracting areas this way makes equality of figures what is now called *equidecomposability*, or, with subtraction allowed, *equicomplementability*.

---
title: Triangles by their angles
---
A triangle is *right-angled* if it has a right angle, *obtuse-angled* if it has an obtuse angle, and *acute-angled* if all three angles are acute.

That these three classes cover all triangles without overlap is a theorem: by [[1.17]], any two angles of a triangle are less than two right angles, so a triangle has at most one angle that is right or obtuse.
